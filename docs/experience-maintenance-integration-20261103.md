Complete the four reviewed integration gaps: placement metadata through contribution and curation; source-versus-candidate prompt instructions; retired-entry navigation; and catalog/evidence checks. Inspect the existing maintenance instructions and give future contributors concrete writing references from their own frozen library. No new scheduler, automatic RSI, generation, or independent scoring.

## Implementation

1. Carry `layer`, `tracks`, `families`, and `stages` through contribution creation, linked comments, explicit curation, navigation, search and frozen snapshots. Retain v1 compatibility and task/contributor/curator authority boundaries.
2. Follow Opus's input-specific prompting: a source restart carries the complete desired construction; a compatible candidate continuation states the new change and a short preservation clause. Align shared manuals without weakening experience prose.
3. Repair active historical reading links and document the fifteen old entry destinations. Historical run snapshots remain unchanged.
4. Check explicit held-out provenance in JSON and text, validate placement metadata, and compare generated catalog/navigation with checked-in files in `--check` mode.
5. Give maintenance the current library's representative entries and clear writing guidance: lead with a practical judgment, explain the construction or mechanism, give a usable action, and put evidence in a compact footer. Keep model-led reading and prose; do not add a semantic grader or mandatory reasoning form.

## Maintenance behavior and writing alignment

The existing path remains: an eligible settled training task opens its family's nonempty full private library, even if the original online arm used no experience. The model reads saved requests, actual prompts, inputs and outcomes, uses the maintenance Skills, and submits prose plus optional append-only contributions. Campaign policy controls ingestion. An explicit curator can later accept, merge, supersede or decline; task contributors still cannot edit canonical text, manuals or Skills.

The maintenance prompt now asks the model to read the nearest current account and a representative entry before drafting. Its initial and continuing context expose three writing-reference IDs, their current private versions/hashes, their purpose and a ready-to-use read request. The examples are P-every-plane (construction judgment), B-emission-binding (a precise renderer-specific relation), and C-long (task-conditioned reasoning), with same-layer fallbacks for another library. The examples are resolved from the bound snapshot, not today's repository.

The shared authoring standard and all three Skills align on direct recommendations, explanations of the visual construction or renderer behavior, concrete application and compact evidence attribution. Comments can make one useful correction; a new entry explains a distinct judgment for a reader who did not perform the task. This is semantic guidance, not a compulsory outline or prose grading program. Existing read/contribution records support later use review; style adherence and quality still require examining actual future contributions.

The repository builder and private curator have distinct roles: the builder assembles reviewed file-backed material for publication; private maintenance uses the existing contribution/curation interfaces. Neither path automatically grants repository or Skill-editing permission. No extra maintenance model, autonomous adoption phase or scheduling system was added.
