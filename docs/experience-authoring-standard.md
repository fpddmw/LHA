# Writing judgment-bearing experience

Shared by the three maintenance Skills. Purpose: turn development traces into practical understanding that improves video-editing decisions. The [complete library](../knowledge/staged/experience-full-maintenance-20261101/README.md) is the current writing reference; the earlier four examples remain historical drafts.

## Experience takes responsibility for a judgment

An entry earns attention when it helps Planner notice a consequential condition, explain an outcome, and prefer a promising course over a plausible trap. State the preference clearly. A capable model gains little from an event list or from being told that every option has risks.

- Event: a robe hid a chair; a later jacket exposed it.
- Useful judgment: the authored silhouette covered the support relation that made sitting readable. Change that silhouette or its placement before adding more preservation wording. If the garment is user-required, design its drape around the necessary contact evidence.
- Weak conclusion: both clothing and preservation matter; inspect and decide.

A strong entry changes what the reader notices, proposes, compares or stops repeating. The value can lie in a better initial design as well as a repair. Preserve the user's creative objective when recommending a lower-risk realization.

## Give working defaults under uncertainty

Development evidence can support a useful conditional preference before a controlled comparison exists. Explain why that preference fits the observed problem and what would change it.

For example, useful foreground appearance and an unfinished environment favor continuing a candidate whose remaining spatial transformation is compatible with that foreground. Broken identity or activity favors an input that still contains the original relationship, together with a reconsidered design. The state difference is central to the decision, even though it also limits a causal comparison of routes.

State a working explanation at the level the evidence supports, then develop its practical consequence. When multiple explanations remain plausible, identify the observation that separates them and recommend the most useful next action. Keep a proposed repair visibly distinct from an observed repair by saying so once, in ordinary prose.

Counterexamples should sharpen the advice. F004's below-neck instruction accompanied a better face judgment; F002 used that boundary and still failed. Recommend reducing unnecessary reconstruction around faces and keeping the intended clothing explicit. A boundary phrase is a way to express that design, rather than a substitute for it.

A default has a reason and an exception. Repeated explicit failure lowers the value of wording-only retries; a credible alternative realization or a justified sampling attempt can still be worth trying. Avoid converting this judgment into a fixed number of attempts.

## Seek distinctions that change a proposal

Useful analytical lenses include:

- **Omission versus scope conflict versus execution failure:** a missing orientation, a request that preserves a playing surface while globally replacing the table, and an explicitly requested displacement that never appears call for different revisions.
- **Instance correspondence versus category construction:** more facial adjectives can specify a plausible new person; retaining the source individual requires attention to input evidence and competing transformations.
- **Authored conflict versus weak emphasis:** costume, light or scenery can cover the contacts that make an action intelligible. Revise the conflicting realization.
- **Activity relations versus an object inventory:** a microphone's recipient or a tile wall's role distinguishes the event from another plausible scene with the same objects.
- **Connected visual change versus verbal locality:** a window, its view, frame and reflected light can form one coherent edit even though several objects change.
- **Visible motif versus structural realization:** embroidery, lamp objects and sci-fi accents can appear while a required collar, illumination pattern or environment remains absent.

Use these lenses to discover a useful account. Let a different distinction emerge when the records call for one.

## Write for progressive reading

The title and discovery summary should already communicate the condition and practical implication. Open the entry with its operative judgment. Develop the mechanism or explanatory distinction, the cues that make it relevant, and the actions it favors. Keep examples specific enough to make those actions imaginable. Use structure suited to the phenomenon rather than filling the same template in every entry.

Keep decision-changing conditions beside the recommendation: requested wide sleeves, a close-up whose expression is the point, a renderer-specific failure, or an input that has already lost a face. Put detailed counts, protocol history, coverage and non-isolation notes in a compact **Evidence** footer. A limitation that changes the recommended action belongs in the main explanation; repeating general uncertainty throughout the entry consumes attention without improving judgment.

Renderer profiles should provide actionable starting preferences and link to fuller entries. Professional manuals teach the general reading and reasoning methods; entries own revisable empirical claims. Keep stable tool facts in the operating manual. This lets a new observation update one account instead of leaving contradictory copies across manuals.

## Ground the interpretation in the actual intervention

Compare the original request, selected input, exact submitted prompt and observed result. Check the clauses that would distinguish omission from an unsuccessful explicit instruction. F005 v1 already preserved the green playing surface and tiles; its later improvement concerns clearer scope allocation, not merely adding a forgotten noun. F002 v3 retained explicit tuxedo instructions; its failure cannot be explained as deleting that requirement.

Read primary records for consequential claims. Use exact quotation for exact text and identify illustrative prompt wording as an example. Open frames or video for new visual observations; attribute saved reviews when using their observations. Record counts deterministically with explicit units. Keep online reviews, independent scores and human inspection identifiable in the evidence footer. Unknown remains an observation gap, with useful local evidence retained.

Usage review should inspect what was available, what Planner said, what it actually did, and what happened. A failed generation can follow a sensible decision; a success can occur without the entry. These alternatives help decide whether to change discovery, wording, the underlying explanation or neither. Recommend exact edits when the record reveals a useful improvement.

## Maintain and stage

Merge duplicated explanations and split claims whose conditions lead to different actions. Preserve evidence, old IDs and replacement history. Use authorized development material; keep held-out outcomes outside maintenance input. Stage library and Planner-manual changes for content review and activation approval. Maintenance Skills may be revised within the user's authorized scope. Current task snapshots stay fixed.

The [usage interface](experience-usage-interface.md) supplies factual records and batch exports. Analysis, use review and curation are separately usable maintenance capabilities; select the ones the current question needs.
