# Skills, experience and task workspace: agreed direction

Date: 2026-11-01.

Status: originally recorded as an agreed direction. The subsequent [bounded implementation plan](experience-library-refactor-plan.md) was approved on 2026-11-01 and now governs implementation. Three maintenance Skills, optional professional manuals and passive provenance implement that direction. Four new experience examples remain staged for content review; no RSI or experiments are authorized. The open questions below preserve the discussion history and are addressed by the newer plan and [interface](experience-usage-interface.md).

Related material: [current development plan](creative-loop-development-plan.md), [creative knowledge map](../knowledge/creative-loop/README.md), [staged E1](../knowledge/staged/e1-20261030/README.md).

## Purpose

Support two connected Planner abilities: translating the request and source video into a coherent visual proposal; and interpreting actual results to make useful revisions and delivery choices. Tools and records support those abilities. More documents, mandatory forms or additional agent roles are not objectives.

Understanding narrative emphasis, actions, semantic relationships and task priorities remains central. Removing a separate semantic Skill category must not remove that capability. An ordinary activity need not acquire an invented story. Planner should understand which visible relationships make the activity intelligible and which changes the user actually requests or permits.

## Three manuals and their material

The agreed direction is one concise operating manual and two on-demand professional manuals for the same Planner. These are available resources, not three mandatory phases.

| Manual | Responsibility | Material it helps Planner use |
|---|---|---|
| Operating manual | Task ownership, tool use, candidate identity, actual renderer inputs, evidence limits, one-shot Critic collaboration, persistence and delivery | Tool contracts and shared score definitions |
| Understand the task and design a proposal | Interpret events and relationships; distinguish requirements, observations and authored additions; compare and adapt visual designs | Design library, semantic/design guides, complete cases, relevant experience |
| Understand results and revise the proposal | Interpret findings and uncertainty; examine failure explanations; judge expression, scope, input and candidate tradeoffs | Experience cards, renderer observations, actual task evidence and relevant design material |

The design library retains its map, thematic comparisons, complete source accounts and references. The current meaning guide belongs among those thematic resources and supports the design manual. It is not discarded.

Experience can matter before the first generation; design knowledge can matter during a later revision. Manuals guide interpretation and application without fixing creative content, reading quotas, diagnosis forms or a reasoning sequence. Critic retains its separate frozen scoring manual and supplies observations and scores, not optimization suggestions. Planner forms the proposed changes.

## Experience cards

A useful card carries a conditional understanding that can change interpretation or action: the phenomenon, a meaningful explanation, decision implications, supporting observations and limits. Mechanism hypotheses remain distinguishable from observations and established interface facts. A raw event summary or generic advice is insufficient by itself.

Cards connect to actual inputs, prompts, outcomes and evaluation provenance, with visual comparisons where they clarify the claim. The complete evidence can remain behind a concise readable account. Exact card structure, metadata and authoring procedures are not yet decided.

The relationship is many-to-many. One experience may inform semantic understanding, expression and input selection. Maintain the connected understanding rather than copying separate rules into each manual. Design sources explain authored visual relationships; experience explains what happened when particular generators attempted related work. A generator failure does not rewrite the original design source.

The existing staged E1 contains 45 cards in seven topics:

| Topic | Cards |
|---|---|
| Continue or stop | S1-S6: 6 |
| Respond to unmet requirements | R1-R7: 7 |
| Choose an editing input | I1-I5: 5 |
| Express edits | X1-X7: 7 |
| Inspect and compare | C1-C4: 4 |
| Bernini profile | B1-B7: 7 |
| Ditto profile | D1-D9: 9 |

These are five decision-oriented entrances and two renderer profiles, not seven independent Skills. They mainly cover diagnosis and revision; their coverage of initial event interpretation and task meaning is partial. The topics need not form a closed taxonomy.

The draft contains useful interpretations, historical observations, operating conventions and proposed policies. It also retains incompatible old instructions: Critic process-context diagnosis, all-contender Critic requests, intent and ledger assumptions. Its content needs curation before any new integration. Its counts and conclusions retain their historical scope; this discussion did not re-evaluate the cited videos. E1 remains staged and excluded from the current creative runtime.

## Workspace, notes and operation records

| Element | Producer | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Operation records | Harness automatically | Preserve what was read, observed, submitted and returned, with actual input ancestry |
| Notes | Planner | Retain useful current findings, questions and tradeoffs |
| Workspace | Planner | Organize the current task interpretation, alternatives and evidence through editable summary/nodes/links |
| Experience library | A separately determined curation process | Supply reusable understanding beyond this task |

The implemented workspace is a persistent, editable task-local container. It does not automatically turn logs into reliable understanding, validate its own hypotheses, or publish cross-task knowledge. Logs and workspace are distinct parts of task state. Planner notes are not hidden reasoning transcripts; concise judgments and supporting evidence are enough.

The intended connection is: manuals help Planner interpret a question; knowledge and experience supply relevant material; Planner applies it to the current video and can retain its judgment in the workspace; new observations can revise that judgment. Actual operations remain independently traceable. A later experience account may use these materials, but a task note does not automatically become a general rule.

## Questions reserved for the next discussion

The priority is how useful experience is produced and maintained, then the smallest implementation that supports it:

1. **Generation:** what initiates experience formation, who performs it, and whether it happens during work, after a task or across a group of tasks. How to obtain explanatory, transferable understanding rather than summaries or generic advice, without imposing a reflection ritual on Planner.
2. **Organization:** what belongs in a card, a thematic synthesis, a stable manual or the underlying evidence. How related observations, contradictions and alternative explanations stay connected without duplicated rules.
3. **Archiving and revision:** how to retain original outcomes and interpretation history, revise or retire claims, and distinguish task-local ideas from cross-task material. No publication or approval mechanism is selected here.
4. **Use through Skills:** how Planner discovers relevant experience, reads deeply when useful and applies it without treating a recommendation as an instruction. Determine which small entrances are visible by default and which material is retrieved on demand.
5. **Workspace linkage:** how current judgments refer to observations and reads, how obsolete task hypotheses are replaced, and what evidence can later support experience formation. Automatic factual logging and model-authored interpretation keep distinct roles.
6. **Implementation and evaluation:** settle file/module changes and access boundaries after the above decisions. Preserve the ability to compare fixed manuals/tools with and without experience. Direct versus Loop-first measures the joint planning contribution; Loop-final versus Loop-first measures subsequent iteration and selection, with actual costs and incomplete outcomes retained.

No new manual, card format, automatic extraction pipeline, memory publication process or experimental arm is implemented by this record. The next discussion should resolve the experience lifecycle and its use in ordinary Planner work before expanding infrastructure.
