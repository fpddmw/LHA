## Objective and evidence role

Use the completed runs as development evidence to identify and repair failures in planning, feedback, knowledge use, candidate decisions, and execution support. Existing runs establish operational integration and mixed task outcomes; they do not establish a reliable overall quality gain. Preserve all original results, failed attempts, frozen configurations, and missing evaluations. Further scoring should be selected for its diagnostic value after review, rather than performed to fill every old table.

First improve the prototype and verify its behavior on a small, informative set. Larger experiments follow a reviewed assessment of quality, generalization, uncertainty, and cost. Tasks used to develop experience remain identifiable as development material.

## Two levels of RSI

Task-level RSI updates the current task's understanding and strategy from observed results. It may change the visual proposal, expression, editing input, observation strategy, knowledge use, or final candidate choice. A further generation is an action; an evidence-responsive change in subsequent decisions is the intended self-improvement mechanism. Retain useful findings, failed assumptions, and unresolved questions in lightweight task memory, without requesting private reasoning transcripts or mandatory diagnostic forms.

Global RSI turns task evidence into scoped proposals for improving reusable decision experience and working methods. It requires executable evidence collection, proposal preparation, isolated validation, reviewed adoption, version tracking, and rollback. Existing proposal/publication components should be reused. A task's provisional interpretation does not automatically become shared knowledge. Stable tool contracts and runtime code change through an explicit development/review path.

## Architecture principles

- The Planner remains model-led: it understands the source/request, identifies information needs, reads and combines knowledge, chooses a proposal and editing input, and decides when to compare or stop.
- Harness code supplies reliable operations, provenance, candidate access, context continuity, resource limits, and recoverability. It does not prescribe aesthetic mappings, a fixed creative tree, mandatory reading quotas, or route-approval forms.
- Preserve the knowledge map, comparative guides, complete entries, and provenance. Improve their use for actual task questions. Distinguish semantic/design knowledge, operating guidance, revisable decision experience, and task-local memory.
- Support both short, open requests and long, specific requests. Concrete visible style features are a supported development direction. No universally reliable semantic-preservation wording has been established.
- Online Critic findings should help the Planner understand attainment, defects, changes, tradeoffs, and uncertainty. Failure-cause explanations remain hypotheses unless supported by discriminating evidence.
- Candidate decisions need intelligible, evidence-linked explanations, particularly for consequential changes of editing input. Keep all candidates available; do not force source restarts, immediate-parent editing, fixed round counts, or latest-candidate selection.
