# Understand the task and form a visual proposal

Use this manual when interpretation or design needs clarification. It is an optional resource for the same Planner, not a required planning phase.

A video contains relationships through which an activity becomes intelligible: a person supported by a chair while reading, a hand acting on an object, players responding to a ball. Distinguish what the user requests, what the visible source establishes, and what you choose to add. Ordinary actions need not become a new narrative. A genre label does not require assigning a new role to every object.

The [meaning guide](../guides/meaning.md) develops actions, object roles, viewpoint and information order through complete cases. Use it to understand which relations matter to this task. User priorities and permitted changes guide the proposal; neither an inventory of every source detail nor an invented story defines those priorities automatically.

Explore [theme](../guides/theme.md), [media](../guides/media.md), [time](../guides/time.md), [combination](../guides/combination.md) and [design expression](../guides/design-expression.md) when a visual direction needs development. Read complete entries for how marks, materials, lighting, space and staging produce the effect. Adapt the relationship that is useful rather than copying a case's objects, palette or plot.

A detailed request may already provide the realization. A short request may still be precise, or leave room for alternatives. Concrete features can make a chosen direction legible, as in the [historical Bernini probe](../model-experience/bernini-r.md), but extra length is not itself a strategy and that probe did not establish reliable preservation wording.

The [experience entrance](../experience/curated/README.md) can reveal a relevant rendering condition before the first attempt. Read an entry when its question matches a consequential uncertainty. Consider the conditions and evidence behind its explanation, including the renderer and actual interface. An experience can change how you understand an option without dictating which option to choose.

Keep useful current distinctions or alternatives in optional notes/workspace. A concise reference to an entry can clarify why it mattered, but citation is optional. The workspace holds your interpretation of this task; the reading tools preserve the source material separately. Generate when the proposal is worth trying, then use its actual result to revisit the understanding.
