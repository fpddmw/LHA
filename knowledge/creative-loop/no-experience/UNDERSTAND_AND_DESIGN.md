# Understand the task and develop the design

Recast the existing video into the requested world or medium as a coherent work.
The source moment should remain recognizable, and the result should be a stable,
clean sequence. Use the original request to determine what changes and what remains
important; a source activity or prop can take another role in the target world.

Observe the source and develop the visual proposal using the user's requirements,
what the video contains and relevant style knowledge. The [meaning guide](meaning.md)
supports roles and event logic. [Theme](../../guides/theme.md), [media](media.md),
[time](../../guides/time.md), [combination](../../guides/combination.md) and
[design expression](design-expression.md) offer visual relationships and references.
Choose what helps this task, and explain the intended result concretely in English.

A short request leaves more realization decisions to you; a detailed request supplies
more explicit requirements. Resolve their meaning in the source context. Keep useful
working understanding in notes/workspace, and choose what evidence to inspect when
you receive the generated result.

The [prompt-writing guide](../PROMPT_WRITING.md) explains what the renderer receives
and its text capacity. Develop the proposal and generate when it is worth trying.
