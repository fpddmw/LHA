# Plan an attempt around the decision it can change

Use the original task and the strongest retained evidence to decide what deserves another generation. A generation may construct a new realization, recover information from an earlier input, or sample a plausible alternative. State which of these purposes matters here and choose the actual conditioning video accordingly.

[Continuation value](../experience/curated/entries/continuation-value.md) helps judge a consequential unresolved target gap. [Realization change](../experience/curated/entries/realization-change.md) distinguishes a new construction from another description of the same operation. The [input account](../experience/curated/entries/input-conditioning.md) explains when a useful candidate or earlier input supports that construction. Read their conditions and contrasting cases before treating a successful historical choice as a default.
