# Plan an attempt around the decision it can change

Use the original task and the strongest retained evidence to decide what deserves another generation. A generation may build a plane or counterpart that is missing, recover information from an earlier input, or sample an alternative. State which of these purposes matters here and choose the conditioning video accordingly.

Spend attempts on a different construction, input or scope, not a restatement ([spending attempts](../experience/curated/entries/P-spend-attempts.md)). Keep earlier candidates as options and compare them all before delivery ([compare retained](../experience/curated/entries/P-compare-retained.md)). Read the [renderer profile](../experience/curated/renderers.md) before treating a successful historical choice as a default.

The [shared operating reference](OPERATING_REFERENCE.md) explains continuation reminders and deadline settlement.
