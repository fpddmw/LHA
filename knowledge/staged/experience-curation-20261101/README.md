## Renderer entrances

**Bernini-R:** all four demonstrations contain Bernini observations. They concern the recorded 1.3B/40-step APG setup, with exact settings retained in source receipts. The connected-scope alternative is untested. Identity observations include difficult near-person edits; they do not establish a universal tradeoff.

**Ditto:** instance identity includes the recorded VACE/Ditto fewshot runs; input conditioning includes a geometry-repair counterexample. These observations are primarily historical online judgments. They do not inherit Bernini's independent scores or establish that one route is always preferable.
