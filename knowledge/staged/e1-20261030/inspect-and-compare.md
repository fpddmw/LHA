# Inspect and compare results

[Decide to continue or stop](decide-to-continue-or-stop.md) · [Meaning](../../guides/meaning.md)

Version E1 (staged, replaces v1).

### C1 · Select by core requirements and major damage first
When: choosing the final candidate.
Do: rank contenders first by unmet core user requirements and major unrequested damage, then by style. A candidate without major damage usually beats one with stronger style plus a broken identity, action or structure, unless the request is mainly a style request.
Why: B/FS4-no selected v3, the only candidate without major damage on the independent evaluation. B/024L-no selected v1, the only one that passed.
Status: observed · any renderer

### C2 · Compare every real contender before finishing
When: more than two candidates exist and the decision is close.
Do: request one review comparing all contenders, or inspect them yourself with a multi-video `observe`. Do not finish from a pairwise review that omitted a contender.
Why: several final reviews could not rank all candidates because earlier ones were not supplied (B/001L e0006, D/027S-no e0007, D/FS4-full e0010). B/FS4-full finished with v2 after a v3-versus-v2 review; v1 had higher independent edit (0.72 vs 0.39) and style (3.1 vs 2.8) scores but was not in that comparison.
Status: observed

### C3 · Check a consequential claim yourself before spending a generation
When: a review claim would trigger another generation, such as a reshaped landmark or a lost accessory.
Do: inspect the relevant source/candidate evidence when it could change the decision, reusing attached overviews when sufficient. Use recorded frame correspondence for motion. Clear evidence needs no ritual reinspection.
Why: in this batch the Planner requested three additional observations in two loops, while source/latest-result overviews were attached automatically; D/027S-full e0008–e0009 used crops before the last attempt.
Status: operating convention

### C4 · Unknown is not failure
When: a review leaves a requirement unknown (fine motion, small objects at the available resolution).
Do: do not generate to resolve an unknown. Inspect if it matters, or report it.
Status: operating convention
