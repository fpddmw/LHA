# Seven-task maintenance: what changed in our understanding

Date: 2026-11-01. This is a human-initiated analysis/use-review/curation pass over saved development evidence. No new rendering, model scoring, simulated Planner run, or automatic knowledge publication occurred. All proposed content remains staged.

## Material and coverage

All 45 old cards were read, along with their evidence index and relevant original prompts/reviews. The package includes hash-bound extracts of the 23 historical runs behind that index and seven new fewshot runs. The existing batch exporter was actually run on the seven explicitly listed development runs; the export is at `out/experience_curation/20261101/fewshot-seven-maintenance/`. The seven-run extracts preserve exact prompts, input ancestry, recorded execution settings, notes and online reports. Selected local frames were opened and copied unchanged with video/frame hashes; [inspection scope](evidence/visual-inspection.md) is explicit.

The seven tasks produced 21 candidates and 21 completed online reviews, with seven retained selections. All seven stop reasons were budget; none establishes autonomous delivery satisfaction. They ran the old RSI snapshot with Planner-authored requirements and automatic multi-call Critic, not today's creative-loop protocol. Online judgments below belong to that historical protocol. There is no independent final evaluation for this batch. Fewshot 004 also appeared in older development evidence; these are development cases, not a held-out generalization result.

## Judgments added or revised

1. **Activity identity resides in relations.** F003/F005 motivate a new entry about genre-compatible reconstruction of who does what. This goes beyond observing 'poses changed': it explains why preserving a microphone, guitar, players or table can leave the activity wrong. Explicit relational wording remains fallible, as F003 v2 shows.
2. **Continuation preserves a platform; it does not establish the next edit's value.** F004/F006/F007 refine input-conditioning experience. The three candidate continuations all started from no-major-damage v2s; the four source restarts began with damaged v2s. Their outcome difference is conditioned on that initial choice. F004 directly separates preservation success from edit gain.
3. **Environment identity needs spatial carriers.** The profile claim 'Bernini under-applies environment' becomes a distinction between motifs and the surfaces, boundaries and sightlines that define a place. F001 and F004 v1 prevent treating incomplete environments as a universal Bernini property. F006/F007 provide useful, still confounded, spatial-realization examples.
4. **Lighting is relational, not a lamp inventory.** Repeated chandelier/cyan-accent successes with incomplete room lighting motivate a separate entry about emitters, receivers, fill and reflection. This also reorganizes the older Ditto color-cast material.
5. **Persistence and object continuity are separate from recognizable appearance.** F006's embroidery and F005's changing suit refine temporal interpretation. Ball/phone failures motivate an additional hypothesis about continuity across occlusion, possession and release. These provide better inspection and proposal questions, not proven repair formulas.
6. **The four demonstrations become more discriminating.** Design conflict now distinguishes optional robes from user-required wide sleeves and from relational failures. Identity now includes F004/F006 counterexamples and the failure of below-neck wording on F002. Connected scope includes both spatially explicit gains and F004's failed background completion. Input conditioning includes selection bias and stable-but-unproductive continuation.

## What the counts support

[Recount script](evidence/recount.py) and [facts](evidence/batch-facts.json) reproduce the seven-run descriptive counts. Four of seven selections are earlier candidates. Nine of fourteen later attempts have recorded collateral regressions. Known generation time is 14,571.66 seconds across all 21 returned candidates (about 4.05 GPU-hours), excluding LLM time, setup and failed/unreturned work.

Third attempts from v2: three attempts, three without reported major damage, two with at least one known ordinal requirement improvement. Third attempts from source: four attempts, zero without major damage, two with an ordinal improvement (F001 action and F003 guitar). An earlier account's 'one of four improves' uses a different or unspecified notion of improvement; the exact per-item recount here shows why that number must not be called overall quality gain. F003 v3 still lost to its v1 selection. No scalar quality score is constructed.

All 21 intents targeted every requirement. Saved item checks total 64 hit, 66 miss and 24 unknown; first-attempt checks derive target attainment while later checks use declared comparisons. These do not form a calibrated prediction score and are not imported into the new harness. Temporal requirements produce 13 fail, one partial and 40 unknown across 54 judgments. Positive local observations remain visible even when the whole-clip verdict is unknown.

## Review of actual experience use

[Usage audit](evidence/usage-audit.json) inspected the 51 saved Planner call inputs, their hashes and structurally returned read texts. Every inspected input's digest contains the same three Wiki concept IDs. No E1 S/R/I/X/C/B/D card was in the saved experience catalog. This was not a test of the 45-card library.

Returned reads by task are 1, 4, 3, 0, 0, 5 and 1. Those counts include navigation and design knowledge. F002 read two Wiki concepts and two static guidance pages before v3; its note explicitly framed the shorter refit as an unvalidated hypothesis, and v3 regressed. F006 read Chinese-period material, xianxia, the experience map and static input/expression guidance; its notes linked the v2 choice to pavilion structure and absence of an observed duplicate phone, and v3 gained mountains/costume. F004 selected an appropriate retained candidate without reading any page. F007 had only an experience-map read and still developed a concrete continuation.

The new passive exposure receipt did not exist in those historical calls. This pass instead records exactly what is inspectable in the saved input, without manufacturing old receipts or inferring attention. The notes and later actions are compatible with use of guidance in some cases; they do not isolate its contribution. The maintenance consequence is specific: replace weak topic labels with explanatory summaries, retain successful and failed applications together, and prioritize renderer/scene distinctions beyond advice the Planner already demonstrated. No automatic usefulness score is assigned.

## Deliberately rejected conclusions

- No rule that source restarts are worse: their inputs and tasks were already more damaged/difficult.
- No reliable 'below the neck', shorter-prompt, detailed-identity or positive-only wording recipe.
- No universal ban on candidate geometry repair or negative instructions.
- No assertion that unknown temporal judgments establish motion failure or successful preservation.
- No claim that unused E1 cards caused the decisions, or that the updated library has improved live performance.
