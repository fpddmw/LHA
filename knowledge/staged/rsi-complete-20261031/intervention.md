# Distinguish a new realization from another description

Status: human-assisted judgment draft; its effect on retry quality is unvalidated.

Compare the image operation implied by the old and proposed prompts. Changing “strips”
to “mosaic” may still ask the renderer to displace the same scene-bearing rectangles.
A substantive difference can instead change which surface carries the effect, remove
an occluder or conflicting instruction, alter the temporal relation, or use input content
that makes the intended relation available. Explain the concrete difference if it matters.

Rewording or resampling can still be reasonable. Their opportunity is different from a
changed construction; they do not need to be disguised as a novel mechanism. After several
attempts leave an explicit requirement unrealized, weigh the remaining concrete opportunity
against the best deliverable and observed damage, rather than treating a new phrase as
evidence of a new capability. Stopping and input choice remain yours.

Read [[wiki-realization|realization changes]] and
[[case-ditto-024-before-v3|the overlapping glitch proposals]]. The replay shows overlapping
proposals, not proof that every subsequent retry must fail. EC-intervention tests whether
recorded explicit failures are repaired by a proposed change; it does not assign value
to an untried branch or infer causal credit from a card citation.
