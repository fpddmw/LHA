# A repair has interacting visual carriers

Status: human-assisted mechanism hypothesis; no controlled rendering comparison yet.

An apparently small edit may depend on several connected relations. Table-edge light
is carried by a contour, an emitting material and nearby illumination. Book readability
depends on page separation, light and overlap with hands or clothing. A robe can cover
chair support without the chair having been deleted. These alternatives imply different
interventions and different useful inputs.

Describe a coherent target state for the relations that carry the effect. Inspect
whether an authored glow or garment competes with that state before simply strengthening
preservation wording. A still-legible earlier candidate may retain useful scene information;
a source restart may supply information already obscured in later candidates. Choose
according to the actual information and next realization, retaining previous results as
delivery options.

This focus is not a mask: the current video-plus-text renderer may change its surroundings.
