# Subject matter and visual language

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Video meaning](meaning.md) · [Media](media.md) · [Combination](combination.md)

Choose a visual language for what it makes the viewer notice. The cases below connect subjects to materials, viewing traditions and ways of organizing information. Their differences help select a direction that serves the current request.

## How form connects to a subject

In [The Bridge on the River Kwai](../entries/opuscar-030.md), engineering blueprints directly connect to building a bridge. Dimensions, structure, lines, and red-pencil notes become the language of events. [The Lost Weekend](../entries/opuscar-018.md) makes a similar connection through typewriter character art: an activity or object in the subject supplies a method for forming the image. [Gandhi](../entries/opuscar-055.md) turns the significance of homespun cloth into the logic of a whole printed textile, within which salt, procession, and spinning wheel unfold.

Connections can also arise from social space and viewing traditions. [The Last Emperor](../entries/opuscar-060.md) combines gongbi color painting, ruled architecture, and ceremony. Line and color layers distinguish dress and buildings; composition and ordered figures make ritual readable. [CODA](../entries/opuscar-094.md) uses a maritime craft support and a lateral move between signing and singing. Local life, representational form, and action offer the transferable relationship. Material and historical terms are the source author's references; specific cultural claims require task-appropriate verification.

## Related subjects can use different languages

The war-related cases already offer several directions. [All Quiet on the Western Front](../entries/opuscar-003.md) organizes grief through woodcut masses, cuts, and quiet posture. [Platoon](../entries/opuscar-059.md) uses pen washes and shorthand traces for field recording. [Patton](../entries/opuscar-043.md) relates a vast flag to a speaker in a public performance. [The Hurt Locker](../entries/opuscar-082.md) connects technical diagrams with progressively closer views. Viewpoint, information density, and emotional relationships differ despite related subjects.

Historical figures also receive different treatments. [A Man for All Seasons](../entries/opuscar-039.md) establishes a sitter through formal portrait detail, materials, and gestures. [Shakespeare in Love](../entries/opuscar-071.md) extends a miniature from a private pendant to public theater. [The Last Emperor](../entries/opuscar-060.md) emphasizes ceremony and spatial order. The relationship a proposal seeks to express informs the choice. A period label locates material; a complete explanation establishes its expressive function.

## One visual form can serve different subjects

Technical images have different information roles. [Mutiny on the Bounty](../entries/opuscar-008.md) keeps a voyage on a chart. [Titanic](../entries/opuscar-070.md) develops the same ship from drawing into a 3D world. [Parasite](../entries/opuscar-092.md) reveals spatial levels simultaneously through a section. The first supports a journey on a surface, the second transforms diagram into scene, and the third reveals spaces otherwise difficult to see together. These differences affect what can transfer to a new task.

[Rain Man](../entries/opuscar-061.md) and [A Beautiful Mind](../entries/opuscar-074.md) both add marks to an image. Counting annotations portray attention in one; connections express discovering relationships in the other. Their actions and shots help determine whether marks arise from a person's observation, a narrator's explanation, or a display physically present in the world.

## What the base settings offer

The 13 base templates contain world proposals to develop, but most lack OPUSCAR's fuller connection between narrative and form. [Xianxia](../entries/base-xianxia.md) emphasizes spiritual space, dress, and ritual; [wuxia](../entries/base-wuxia.md) emphasizes martial life and action. [Cyberpunk city](../entries/base-cyberpunk_dystopia.md), [clean laboratory](../entries/base-clean_lab.md), and [industrial wasteland](../entries/base-industrial_wasteland.md) make different assumptions about technology and living conditions.

For cyber-xianxia, choose the relationship technology has to cultivation before accumulating motifs. Technology as ritual infrastructure suggests a different setting from technology as bodily augmentation or oppressive urban control. Use the source activity and the user's specified changes to select a relationship, then develop the materials and spatial organization that make it visible. The base settings supply vocabulary and world assumptions for that choice, rather than assigning a replacement to every object.

## Prefer the form that serves the intended attention

Choose diagrammatic form when exposing structure or connections is central. Choose a situated sketch or tactile mark when presence and human gesture should dominate. Choose ordered portrait or ceremonial arrangements when social position and viewing distance matter. The war and historical-figure comparisons above show why a shared topic can justify different treatments.

A useful transfer preserves the source's expressive relationship while adapting its setting. A blueprint can reveal how an arena is organized; it need not import a bridge. When the user already specifies a medium or world, develop the relevant relationships within that choice. These are design preferences supported by comparison of authored cases, not rankings of renderer performance.

## Carry an interpretation into a proposal

A broad style request leaves room for the model to select which relationships to emphasize. A detailed request supplies more of the world and appearance already. Knowledge can deepen either: the [storybook walkthrough](design-expression.md#benchmark-main041-an-open-storybook-request) chooses a medium and adapts its mechanisms, while the [space-station walkthrough](design-expression.md#benchmark-fewshot004-develop-a-specified-space-station-design) develops materials and local effects within the supplied direction. The amount of invention follows the request and current design questions.
