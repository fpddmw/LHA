# How different media form an image

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Subject relationships](theme.md) · [Time](time.md) · [Combination](combination.md)

Choose a medium by how it forms the image: what carries the marks, how edges and values develop, and how light belongs to the depicted scene or its physical support. The source cases below make these alternatives concrete.

## Paper and pigment participate in form

[An American in Paris](../entries/opuscar-024.md) deliberately offsets ink and transparent color while dance continues the lively relationship. [Ordinary People](../entries/opuscar-053.md) uses reserved paper, transparent glazing, and small autumn changes to carry a gaze. Both are watercolors, but transparency, edges, and unpainted areas have different roles. The opaque masses and mining space of [How Green Was My Valley](../entries/opuscar-014.md) establish another weight through gouache. [Nomadland](../entries/opuscar-093.md) uses pastel edges, a horizon, and fading daylight for sustained viewing.

[Rebecca](../entries/opuscar-013.md) uses tone, erasure, and rubbing for a fogged manor. [The Deer Hunter](../entries/opuscar-051.md) uses fine dry-brush tempera and open composition. Edges, continuous values, and background visibility differ. These distinctions help a model decide how a medium suits a space or mood and how to use it.

## Printing constraints become visual language

[All Quiet on the Western Front](../entries/opuscar-003.md) builds form through black masses and white cuts. The screenprint in [You Can't Take It with You](../entries/opuscar-011.md) emphasizes opaque ink, separations, and coverage. [In the Heat of the Night](../entries/opuscar-040.md) organizes night light through three Risograph colors and reserved paper. Their flat paper surfaces contain different accounts of light, contour, and color interaction.

[Midnight Cowboy](../entries/opuscar-042.md) makes photographic screenprint repetition part of the event, transforming street action into grouped images. Printing is both material and a way of reorganizing a figure. [The Best Years of Our Lives](../entries/opuscar-019.md) first immerses the viewer in deep-focus photographic space, then reveals spread, fold, and text. Related print features can be continuously visible or become new information at the end.

## Materials have different relationships with light

The stained glass in [Going My Way](../entries/opuscar-017.md) consists of transmitting pieces and divisions, projecting color into outer space. [Gladiator](../entries/opuscar-073.md) forms a mosaic through contour-following tesserae, gaps, and reflected light. Both use discrete units, but arrangement, illumination, and viewing position produce different experiences.

Mosaic can also be compared with the pixel art of [Million Dollar Baby](../entries/opuscar-077.md). Tesserae can turn with a contour, while a fixed pixel grid and limited animation organize the game. Rounds and hit stops add a temporal system. Their superficially granular appearances therefore have different structures and timing.

## Photographic appearance and viewing apparatus

[Wings](../entries/opuscar-001.md) combines orthochromatic monochrome, amber tint, projection wear, and aircraft-mounted viewpoints. [The Artist](../entries/opuscar-084.md) uses refined modern production to revisit silent film through monochrome, format, and intertitles. The material offers two distinct silent-film appearances whose features can be compared for a task.

[Marty](../entries/opuscar-028.md) distinguishes an exterior living room from an internal CRT program. [American Beauty](../entries/opuscar-072.md) alternates clean cinema with low-resolution DV, assigning different viewing contexts to them. Scan lines, noise, and screen proportions have positions and boundaries within these complete designs. Readers can establish which image layer a treatment originally belonged to before borrowing it.

## 3D, animation, and spatial expression

[Grand Hotel](../entries/opuscar-005.md) establishes a lobby through a concentric overhead view, then moves to human height. [The French Connection](../entries/opuscar-044.md) uses repeated columns, camera placement, and forward speed for pursuit. Space and movement contribute substantially to the effect. [Around the World in 80 Days](../entries/opuscar-029.md) compresses a journey through geometry, shallow parallax, and limited animation. [It Happened One Night](../entries/opuscar-007.md) instead depends on elastic bodies and timed gags.

The original 2D, 2.5D, and 3D labels describe production methods. Whether a video model can sustain a viewpoint, geometry, or motion depends on its execution capabilities. Use the production idea to choose a visible effect, then express that effect for the available renderer. For example, shallow parallax and graphic shapes imply different spatial treatment from elastic bodies and timed gags.

## Select the construction that matters to this task

For light, open forms, compare transparent wash with visible paper and line. For graphic weight, compare opaque masses, cut marks and separated print colors. For a granular surface, choose whether units follow contours, transmit light or stay on a fixed grid. These choices distinguish watercolor, woodcut, mosaic, glass and pixel treatments more usefully than a shared texture label.

Decide where the medium exists. A CRT inside a photographed room calls for a bounded treatment; turning the whole image into a CRT program changes the viewer's relationship to the entire scene. Likewise, painted light on paper and colored light cast by glass imply different surface and illumination relationships. Name that relationship in the proposal.

## Develop a visible interpretation

The material comparisons can inform a new proposal by explaining how its image forms. Pencil visible through a wash describes the relation between line and color; paper reserved for highlights describes how light is represented; a transparent pane projecting color describes a material in space. Each relation gives the model something it can adapt to a particular object, region, or scene. Source-specific color values and timing remain available for closer reading when relevant.

The [application guide](design-expression.md) develops a storybook interpretation through 053 and 063, then compares material and light choices for a space-station arena. The [Bernini record](../model-experience/bernini-r.md) supplies conditional evidence for concrete style expression. The authored source methods explain designs; the experiment records how particular video-model inputs performed.
