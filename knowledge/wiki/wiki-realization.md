# Rewording and changed visual realization

Status: human-assisted mechanism hypothesis; revised prompts from the replay were not rendered.

A new visual realization changes a relevant relationship, image-forming process or conditioning input. It may remove a conflicting design, change how an effect is spatially carried, or use a candidate that already supplies useful visual structure. A new name for the same requested operation provides weaker evidence of such a change. Rephrasing can still alter a model's response; its value needs measurement.

In [[case-ditto-024-before-v3|Ditto's glitch replay]], the proposed mosaic followed a prompt that already requested image-bearing rectangles and displaced strips. Calling it a different realization left the key question unresolved: what would make scene content move within the blocks rather than produce new opaque overlays? In [[case-bernini-024-before-v3|Bernini's source restart]], transparent echoes and ribbons likewise overlapped earlier demands. These overlaps do not establish that another sample would fail; they limit the evidence for a specific repair mechanism.

By comparison, [[case-bernini-003-before-v3|removing the authored robe]] cancels a concrete occluding design choice. Keeping the fitted garment shape changes the requested relationship between clothing and chair visibility. Its projected benefit was not measured in this replay, but the proposed intervention is identifiable.

Bernini and Ditto receive the selected video and text under their configured renderer interfaces. Writing "composite transparent layers" describes a desired image; it does not invoke a layer compositor or spatial mask. Ground expectations in available controls and the input's visible information.

An explanation of a retry can identify the failed relationship and what the proposal changes using existing intent text. No additional reasoning form is required. When the proposal retains essentially the same visual operation, another attempt is a sampling or phrasing hypothesis; its uncertainty and cost should remain visible. A useful follow-up would compare the relevant prompts on the same input under paired settings.
