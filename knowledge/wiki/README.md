# Judgment concepts and experimental cases

This Wiki connects questions that changed the interpretation of real experiments. Its initial pages are human-assisted, development-exposed knowledge. They are not evidence of automatic RSI improvement. A concept explains a distinction; a case preserves what happened; experience explains a possible decision implication.

- [[wiki-scene-identity|When does a geometric change alter what the scene means?]] Follow the etching case and its unresolved severity disagreement.
- [[wiki-realization|What would make another attempt substantively different?]] Compare actual glitch prompts and proposed alternatives across two renderers.
- [[wiki-connected-change|What relationships would a supposedly local repair affect?]] Examine occlusion, clothing, support and page light in the fantasy study.

Design knowledge remains available through [[media|how media form an image]], [[combination|combining visual methods]] and [[meaning|meaning and requested changes]]. Decision experience is accessible through [[choose-editing-input]], [[express-edits]] and [[inspect-and-compare]].

Double-bracket links are stable knowledge IDs. The read tool resolves them to titles, paths and available tools. Follow any useful link directly; there is no required reading sequence. Image links open archived references through view_reference. They are distinct from observations of the current task.

The proposer may revise concepts and decision experience in a staged version. Historical cases, raw reviews and their image hashes remain fixed. A new interpretation cannot retroactively change the recorded evaluation. Unresolved evidence can become a request for a later observation or experiment.
