# 081 | Slumdog Millionaire: Quiz-Show Studio & Mumbai Orange-Teal

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

A quiz stage alternates with city rooftops, connecting tension and character background through lighting and scale.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Blue-violet beams, answer boxes, audience seating, and a hot seat establish the studio's attention system. Orange-teal rooftops open another space. The source realizes the two treatments in separate shots.

Locking an answer, darkening the room, turning the answer green, and cutting to the city form a specific sequence. Lighting, interface state, and location changes jointly carry the event.

### Original author's design explanation

A hard-lit TV studio in blue-violet with gold highlights, moving-head beams and lens flares, then a hot orange-and-teal Mumbai rooftop. The film cuts between the quiz show's final question and the slums Jamal came from.

### Complete original style prompt

Two registers. Primary, about 70%: a glossy illustrated quiz-show studio, bright and full, seen three-quarter from slightly above: a glass stage lit from below with concentric blue rings, a packed audience behind, moving-head beams cutting blue-violet #5A3FD0 through haze, a big screen of elongated-hexagon answers, one locked in amber #F0A020; bloom and horizontal flares; as tension rises the edges dim to about half, never black. Characters rounded, softly shaded. Second register, about 30%, as full-frame hard cuts: golden-hour views of the subject's home ground, saturated orange and teal, busy (e.g. tin roofs, washing lines, crowds). Accents: magenta #E2206B, teal #0F7C7A, tangerine #F06A14. Camera: push in on screen and hot seat as beams close on one person; elsewhere slow pans. Reference: cinematographer Anthony Dod Mantle (*Slumdog Millionaire*, 2008). Avoid: real show logos; a mostly black studio; even lighting; grey, empty places.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. question | 0.8 seconds

Close on the big screen: the final question with four answer frames, A: ARAMIS locked in amber; a slight push in. No Clawd.

#### 2. tension | 1.0 seconds

Wide of the stage with the host on his stool and Clawd as Jamal in the hot seat: the room darkens and the beams close in on Clawd, then the answer flashes green and the audience reacts; a slow push in.

#### 3. mumbai | 0.8 seconds

Wide of the Dharavi rooftops at golden hour: Clawd stands on a corrugated roof while a packed train passes on the viaduct behind; a slow pan right.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays Jamal: a light-blue check shirt collar and a clip-on mic. The answer is locked from the first frame; the second shot squeezes the room down to the hot seat and releases on the green flash; a hard cut to the Mumbai rooftops then opens the frame up to the city.
