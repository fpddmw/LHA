# Fantasy anime: image layers and epic character setting

A basic fantasy proposal built through legible silhouettes, illustrated depth, and magical light.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

The template treats people as protagonists in a fantasy story. Clothing silhouettes, ornamental rhythm, props, and background depth develop around that assumption. Magical light, metal, and cloth contribute forms and affect how people and surroundings are represented together.

The useful design question is how ornament, silhouette, and environmental light make the chosen fantasy world coherent. A model can develop a calm painted setting, a more graphic character treatment, or another interpretation through additional cases. Protagonist roles and magical props remain choices about the current story.

The material primarily offers a direction; it does not explain different animation methods or particular narrative choices. It can introduce a candidate visual approach, expanded through complete painting, concept-art, and animation cases.
