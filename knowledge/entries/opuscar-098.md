# 098 | One Battle After Another: 3D Dip-Road Chase, Long Lens

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

Telephoto compression makes an undulating road a structure of repeated concealment and reveal; changing viewpoints keep the pursuit readable.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Road waves, narrow sky, heat shimmer, airborne cars, and landing reactions organize speed through alternation between compressed distance and brief nearby events. Strong sun and foreground detail retain object separation.

A telephoto wide view, a driver's backward look, a low crest view, and final tracking provide formation, reaction, and impact. The last raised arm also closes the anthology, a context retained in the record.

### Ingredients

- Real-time 3D (three.js)
- Extreme telephoto compression
- Heat haze and road mirage
- VistaVision fine grain

### Original author's design explanation

A real-time 3D desert road that rises and falls like a roller coaster, three cars chasing over the dips, shot through extreme long lenses with heat haze, bleached sunlight and fine grain. It rebuilds the film's best-known sequence, the dip-road chase.

### Complete original style prompt

Real-time 3D, bleached desert daylight. Signature shot (3–5 s): a straight road over rolling dips and crests at a 2–5° horizontal field of view, stacking dips into waves; the travelling subject sinks and reappears; sky a thin band at most. Then 1–2 s each, a low camera at a crest, a mount on the vehicle or rider. Palette: sky #7fb0e0, haze #ece4d2 only at the far horizon, pale sand #d2ae80 (never orange), hard sun #fff0d8 from high to one side, the main vehicle faded seafoam #86b5ad. Contrast: dark cracked asphalt, crisp shadows, dense green scrub, roadside posts. Default midday; at other hours keep hard sun. Heat shimmer and mirage grow with distance; dust on landings; vehicles leave the ground over crests. Grain ±2%. Reference: Paul Thomas Anderson, *One Battle After Another* (2025). Avoid: wide lenses, a washed-out frame.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. tele | 1.2 seconds

Extreme long-lens shot looking back down the road from far ahead: the dips stacked into waves, the three cars sinking and rising through them, heat shimmer on the asphalt.

#### 2. car | 0.8 seconds

Cut to a hood-mounted shot of Clawd at the wheel of the seafoam convertible, sunglasses pushed up, gripping the wheel then glancing up at the mirror.

#### 3. crest | 0.6 seconds

Cut to a low tripod on the shoulder at a crest: the convertible flies over the top toward the lens.

#### 4. teleBack | 1.1 seconds

Cut to the long lens again, now from behind the pack, the cars dipping out of sight and reappearing over the waves of road.

#### 5. still | 1.3 seconds

Cut to a long-lens tracking shot from ahead: Clawd's convertible leaves the ground over a crest with the dark green and grey sedans closing in behind, lands, and Clawd raises one arm out of the car and holds it to the last frame.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays Bob with a plaid bathrobe collar and gold aviators pushed up on its head, driving the lead car. The chase runs at real speed through five set-ups, alternating long-lens waves with close moments; the last shot ends on Clawd's raised arm, a pose held still so the finale can pick it up.
