# 050 | Annie Hall: New Yorker Cartoon, Ink and Wash

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

A single-panel cartoon's washes, caption, and everyday props become a timed gag, followed by a split-screen comparison of families.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

The kitchen remains fully described while its edges fade into paper. Caricature and restrained washes establish the cartoon tone. A caption is written progressively, joining the lobster, camera, and pot in the joke.

Appearance, photograph, jump, and landing reaction follow one another. The final side-by-side dinner tables introduce another comparative structure. Static-cartoon language can support several temporal arrangements.

### Original author's design explanation

A New Yorker single-panel cartoon: lively nib-pen lines, a few restrained washes, plenty of white paper and a hand-lettered caption beneath. Woody Allen's comedy shares the magazine's voice, and the lobster scene plays as one gag panel.

### Complete original style prompt

New Yorker single-panel cartoon, full-bleed, no border. Nib-pen lines about 1/1000 of frame width on straights, swelling to 1/300 in turns, flicking off at the ends; box corners overlap. Pale watercolour washes with pooled edges over most objects, slipping past the lines by at most 1/150 of frame width: grey #9698A0, sea blue #78A0BE, sand #E2CCA0, rust #C45C32, on cold-press paper #FBF8F1 fading to white at the edges. Exaggerated, expressive figures with drawn faces among everyday props. One hand-lettered caption line under the action, in the user's language, about 1/30 of frame height, appearing letter by letter: a deadpan remark. The drawing holds still; living things wobble. Camera: small drifts and pushes; the frame may split into two side-by-side drawings. References: James Thurber, William Steig, Jean-Jacques Sempé. Avoid: saturated colour, uniform vector lines, a small framed panel floating in white.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. lobster | 0.6 seconds

Extreme close-up on the gap behind the fridge: a lobster slowly peeks out; a slow drift.

#### 2. still | 1.5 seconds

The kitchen: Clawd as Annie laughs and snaps flash photos of Alvy clutching a stockpot; a lobster reaches its foot, it hops, lands laughing, and 'TALK TO HIM. YOU SPEAK SHELLFISH.' writes itself out below; a slight pull back with a handheld wobble.

#### 3. split | 0.4 seconds

The Easter dinner split screen: the quiet Halls on the left with Clawd seated among them, the noisy Singers on the right.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays Annie in Diane Keaton's wide-brimmed hat, loose striped tie and vest, with a 35mm camera. The scene runs at about 1.8× with the jump near 1×: two flash photos, a lobster at its foot, a startled hop, a landing in laughter. Hard cuts; the split-screen dinner is a graphic exit.
