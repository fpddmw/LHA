# Medieval fantasy: material, heraldry, and an inhabited world

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

Stone construction, banners, layered clothing, and worn surfaces organize a fantasy setting grounded in historical materials.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Stone, steel, leather, linen, and old timber supply a physical basis. Torches, embers, overcast light, and fog place characters within it. Heraldry and dress can also express identity and affiliation, to be interpreted within a particular design.

Material wear and lighting can make the proposed world tangible: rough stone receives a different highlight from steel, and woven fabric differs from leather. Developing those relationships gives a period-fantasy proposal visible substance. Heraldic signs, relics, and social identities require choices within the actual request.

The card offers a hybrid fantasy-film proposal without period research or a complete story. Devices, relics, and roles are elements of that proposed world. Related cases allow comparison with other medieval expressions.
