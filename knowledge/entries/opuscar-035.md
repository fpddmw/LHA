# 035 | Lawrence of Arabia: Terpning Epic Poster Gouache

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

Poster gouache uses warm-cool contrast, diagonal action, and desert scale for an epic subject, entered through a graphic match.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Opaque midtone grounds, form-following strokes, and sharp warm highlights create layers. Dry brush is reserved for particular surfaces and distant areas. Scale differences among figures, cloth, and cavalry support the poster composition, connecting painting method to spatial arrangement.

The sunrise inherits the preceding match's position, followed by a charge and scimitar action, then a whip-pan exit. Fixed colors and speed values belong to this implementation. Painting, principal action, and continuity across shots offer a broader relationship to examine.

### Original author's design explanation

Painterly epic-poster gouache with visible directional strokes and a hard warm–cool split: amber sand against cobalt sky. Lawrence of Arabia's original poster was painted by Howard Terpning in exactly this manner.

### Complete original style prompt

1960s hand-painted epic film poster, gouache with oil highlights. Draw forms solidly, then paint: an opaque mid-tone base, directional strokes following each form (1/80–1/50 of frame width on large areas, smaller on faces and hands), soft-blended edges; model every form in three values; dry-brush only on dust, cloth and distant figures; sharp warm-cream highlights last on lit edges. Warm–cool contrast: cobalt sky #1F4D97 to pale blue #A9C3D6, warming to cream near a low sun; ground #EEC27A (any terrain); ochre-red #B56A33 for skin, earth and costume; violet #6C6394 for distant shadows. The hero centred and largest, a diagonal gesture, cloth streaming; smaller, looser figures may recede behind. Light board grain (±3%), warm vignette, haze. Motion: bounce, slow push to a low close-up, a whip pan out. Avoid: scratchy uniform hatching, flat digital fills, cold-only colour.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. sun | 0.7 seconds

Extreme wide of an empty desert at dawn: a huge sun rising through heat haze at frame centre, matched to the blown-out match; slow push in.

#### 2. charge | 1.0 seconds

Poster framing from the camera car: Clawd (Lawrence) gallops in on a camel among charging riders, scimitar low, then raised on the beat with a glint; jolting shake, gentle push.

#### 3. whip | 0.8 seconds

Low angle pushing up to the raised blade, then a whip pan right, out of the frame and into the next film.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays Lawrence: white robes, headcloth and gold agal, a raised scimitar, riding a camel. It opens on the most famous match cut in cinema, West Side Story's match flame becoming the rising sun. The charge plays at a constant 1.5×, the blade goes up on a beat with a glint, and a whip pan hands over to Tom Jones.
