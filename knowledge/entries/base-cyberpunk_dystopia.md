# Cyberpunk dystopia: the original dense-city direction

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

Neon, wet surfaces, devices, and street clothing are situated within a crowded dystopian city.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Signs, reflections, rain, haze, synthetics, and embedded devices interact, allowing people and city to express dense technological life together. The proposal includes physical representation and assumptions about social environment and character.

The source suggests relationships that can be adapted individually: signs color nearby surfaces, wet ground carries reflections, haze reveals distance, and devices participate in clothing or architecture. A new proposal can choose how these work together and whether the source's dystopian intensity serves the request. A combined style can develop a different social or material interpretation.

Cyberpunk may lead to other interpretations in a current task. This material makes its urban, dystopian, and intense-light features available for adoption, revision, or comparison. Restrictions against laboratory or desert mixtures belong to the original template strategy.
