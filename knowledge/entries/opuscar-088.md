# 088 | Spotlight: Microfiche Reader, Newsprint Halftone

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

An archival reading device becomes a mechanism for transitions, connecting journalists' work to the movement of a medium.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Newspaper dots form an image; the microfilm reader's monochrome, scanning, and glass dust add another viewing layer. Concrete scenes remain inside the image instead of every view showing a complete newspaper page.

A newsroom, list, and final front page connect through lateral film movement. The figure changes from journalist to a small character marking the list. Physical space and diagrammatic scale alternate within one reading apparatus.

### Original author's design explanation

A newspaper-halftone picture seen on the green-grey screen of a library microfiche reader, with scan lines, a rolling scan bar and dust on the glass. The reporters dug the story out of archives like this, and the fiche transport replaces the cuts.

### Complete original style prompt

A 2001 newspaper colour halftone on a library microfiche reader. Signature shot: the picture fills the frame, no headline or columns; lines and lettering inside it stay solid. CMYK screens at 15°, 75°, 0°, 45°, dots about 1/250 of frame width, 1 px misregistration. Convert to low-contrast phosphor-green monochrome, dots still clearly visible: film base #1b2320, frame gaps #0c110f, highlights no brighter than #a0dcaf. Only the story's one hero keeps its colour, whole figure (e.g. #D97757), screened and equally low-contrast; every light source turns green. Scan lines every 3 px, a faint scan bar every 4 s, a lamp hot-spot, dust, a hair, a light rounded hood vignette (outer ~5%), a small monospace fiche index. Scene changes slide the fiche sideways past a dark gap (0.5 s); slow push-ins. Avoid: newspaper layout, colour leaking, hard cuts.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. still | 0.8 seconds

Wide on the basement newsroom: reporters round paper-buried desks under fluorescent panels, Clawd in front with a press badge and notepad; slow pull-back.

#### 2. list | 0.8 seconds

The fiche slides sideways to the next frame: top-down on a printed list of priests, a toy-sized Clawd pushing a giant highlighter down the lines while the camera drifts down.

#### 3. headline | 0.9 seconds

Another slide to the loading dock: medium close-up of Clawd beside the front page headline "Church allowed abuse by priest for years", slow push-in.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays a Spotlight reporter: a staff badge on a lanyard, a notepad, later a highlighter. Three frames of fiche, each joined by the reader's sideways transport instead of a hard cut, landing on the front page as the last beat.
