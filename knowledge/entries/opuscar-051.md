# 051 | The Deer Hunter: Andrew Wyeth Egg Tempera

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

Fine dry-brush tempera and an open composition accompany a shot that is never fired; action and gaze reveal a decision.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Low saturation, fine crossing strokes, and a small off-center figure define the landscape. Matte detail, distant mountains, dead grass, and open space organize a quiet view.

Scope, deer eye, lowered gun, and exhalation make not shooting a visible event. The case shows meaning dependent on interrupted action and choice. A list of gun, deer, and mountain would omit that relationship.

### Original author's design explanation

Dry, finely hatched egg tempera after Andrew Wyeth: muted straw, umber and grey-green, grass drawn blade by blade, a pale empty sky. Wyeth was a Pennsylvania painter, and the 'one shot' hunt takes place in the Pennsylvania mountains above a steel town.

### Complete original style prompt

Egg tempera on gesso panel after Andrew Wyeth. Dry, matte, very fine: colour built from dense cross-hatched strokes, each about 1/400 of frame width long, hairline thin. Muted palette: pale overcast sky #D9D5C6, straw grass #CDB27A, umber #6A5234, misty grey-green hills #8E938C, rust #8A4E32; water, stone, clothes and skin mixed from these. One accent colour, only on the one hero subject (e.g. a red cap); any light source is a small pale dot, no halo. Ground (dry grass by default, other ground alike): big light and dark patches, fine strokes, dark tufts, then single wind-combed blades; rocks, buildings hatched along their planes. Empty, lonely space; the subject small, off-centre. Soft overcast light; at other times of day stay pale, no warm glow. Camera: slow drift, quiet push; a circular iris may mark a cut. Avoid: wet watercolour blooms, saturated colour, thick shiny strokes.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. town | 0.6 seconds

Wide on the steel town in the valley mist (blast furnaces, smoking chimneys, an onion-domed church), drifting sideways.

#### 2. scope | 0.7 seconds

Through the rifle scope: the buck on the crest inside a black circular mask and crosshair, swaying slightly with the hunter's hands.

#### 3. deer | 0.3 seconds

Close-up of the buck's head and eye; a slow push.

#### 4. still | 0.8 seconds

Clawd as Michael on a granite outcrop lowers the rifle, shuts its eyes, breathes out and looks up at the buck again, as the camera pulls back to the whole ridge.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays Michael in a red wool hunting cap with a scoped rifle. Valley, scope, eye, hunter: four hard cuts, never faster than about 1.25×, quiet and steady. The beat is the shot not taken: the rifle comes down, eyes shut, one breath out, and it looks up at the buck again.
