# 041 | Oliver!: Victorian Toy Theatre

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

A paper theater exposes its manipulation and viewing apparatus while preserving a complete dramatic exchange within the cardboard space.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Cut edges, parallax between layers, footlight shadows, and a proscenium maintain awareness of a paper stage. Figures perform through motion and spatial relationships specific to this puppet medium.

The wide view shows the tabletop theater and operating hands, then enters the stage and closes on figures. An empty bowl is raised, the other figure shouts, and the first recoils while still holding the bowl. The medium stays visible while the relationship remains readable.

### Original author's design explanation

A Victorian toy theatre: hand-coloured lithographed card figures with a white scissor margin, layered flats on wire slides, a gilt proscenium and flickering candle footlights throwing shadows up the scenery. Oliver! is a musical, staged here on the Victorian home stage.

### Complete original style prompt

Victorian toy paper theatre (Pollock's), all printed card: black lithographic outlines about 1/400 of frame width, sparse crayon hatching on one side of each piece; muted antique hand colouring: deep red #C8262C and dull gold #E0AB36 on proscenium and curtains (never bright yellow), blue #2F5A9A for costumes; scenery, sky, water and skin in greyed browns, greys and cream card #F4ECD6. Every cut-out keeps a white scissor margin (1/200 of frame width). Flats are parallel 2.5D layers in parallax, flat within each plane. Candle footlights throw each cut-out's shadow onto the scenery behind, about 1.5× enlarged, soft, flickering. Gilt proscenium, red drapes and painted audience boxes frame a stage opening 80–90% of frame width. Camera: slow push, lateral tracking, shaky close-ups; a final pull-back shows the dark room. Avoid: bright vector colour, true 3D depth, soft studio light, figures without margins.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. table | 0.5 seconds

Wide: the toy theatre on a mahogany table in a dark parlour, the 'Theatre Royal' proscenium, a sheet of cut-out characters and the player's hand at the side; slow push in.

#### 2. stage | 0.9 seconds

Tracking shot across the stage, the workhouse hall: Clawd (Oliver) steps out of the line of boys and lifts its empty bowl ('Please, sir…') as Mr Bumble's brow gathers by the steaming copper; the camera tracks right with it.

#### 3. close | 1.1 seconds

Close on Clawd and Mr Bumble: Bumble roars, Clawd cowers with a shiver but keeps the bowl up; a small camera shake on the roar.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays Oliver: a cut-out on its own tin slide, holding out an empty bowl. Three clearly different sizes, wide to stage to close, give the request its build: step forward, bowl up on the beat, Bumble's roar, and Clawd still holding the bowl up.
