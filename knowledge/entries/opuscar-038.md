# 038 | The Sound of Music: Storybook 3D Alpine Aerial

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

A storybook-like 3D landscape narrows from aerial scale to a spinning figure, with terrain, wind, and movement curves creating flow.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Rounded ridges, saturated grass, cloud shadows, distant perspective, and foreground grass waves establish a particular landscape. The source combines widescreen photography with storybook meadow imagery, spanning spatial implementation and image treatment.

This case offers a storybook direction through sculpted volume, rounded landforms, and saturated color, distinct from pencil and watercolor on paper. A broad storybook request therefore opens a comparison of visual interpretations. Applying this direction to another setting calls for a new account of its forms and lighting; alpine flight and meadow choreography remain features of this case.

A turn continues across three shots while the camera descends from the air to a low orbit. The case supports comparison of continuous action across cuts and of how viewing distance changes the relationship between figure and nature.

- Miyazaki meadows

### Original author's design explanation

Storybook 3D over procedural Alps: saturated emerald, cobalt and snow white, sculpted ridges, cotton-ball cumulus and gusts running through the grass. It restages the helicopter opening that ends on Maria spinning on the hilltop.

### Complete original style prompt

Storybook 3D landscape in saturated 1960s widescreen colour: smooth sculpted forms, not low-poly or photoreal. Default light: bright sun high, a little behind the camera: emerald grass #3FA33A, cobalt sky #2F6FD0, clean white snow and clouds. Rounded ridges, snow lines following slope, grey-ochre rock bands, cloud shadows. Haze only on the farthest ridges, about 25% lilac. Cumulus are lit white cotton puffs. Knee-high grass and wildflowers fill the foreground, bending in gusts. A lower sun lengthens shadows and warms only cloud edges; other settings keep these colours. Camera: one continuous move: 0–2 s high flight, 2–4 s descent, then a low orbit through the grass around the subject (about 20° per second, subject about 1/4 of frame height). References: cinematographer Ted McCord (*The Sound of Music*, 1965); Miyazaki's meadows. Avoid: violet wash, needle peaks, grey photoreal rock, static grass.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. far | 0.7 seconds

Inside the two binocular circles carried over from My Fair Lady, which open to full frame: a high aerial gliding over snow peaks through cotton-ball clouds.

#### 2. approach | 0.8 seconds

Descent onto the hilltop meadow: Clawd small at frame centre, already turning with carpet bag and guitar case flung out, forest and field patchwork beyond; the camera drops toward it.

#### 3. meadow | 1.0 seconds

Low in the grass, the camera orbits as Clawd twirls a full turn, bag and case swinging, snow peaks and clouds behind, then throws its arms up.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays Maria: an edelweiss and a small headscarf knot, with the poster's striped carpet bag in one hand and a guitar case in the other. The spin is one continuous curve across all three shots, so every cut lands mid-turn, and the arms go up on the last beat.

### References named by the author

Robert Wise, 1965 · Ted McCord (cinematography) · Howard Terpning (1965 poster) · Hayao Miyazaki (meadows)
