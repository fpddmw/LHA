# 059 | Platoon: Combat Artist Field Sketch · Pen & Wash

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

Pen-and-wash field recording combines ground and aerial views into a war fragment centered on infantry experience.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Fast lines, loosely aligned transparent color, paper edges, and handwritten notes establish a field sketch. Visible wet edges and marks coexist with a fully described setting.

Patrol, an overhead landing-zone view, and a return to the figure resemble separate sketch pages, ending on the original poster's gesture. The case supports comparison of field recording, symbolic posture, and film reenactment within one segment.

### Original author's design explanation

Quick, hard pen lines with loose washes of olive, ochre, smoke grey and fire orange on sketchbook paper left bare at the edges. It reads as a war artist drawing on the spot, close to the film's infantryman's view of Vietnam.

### Complete original style prompt

Reportage pen-and-wash field sketch. Pen: warm black, fast confident lines ~1/600 of frame width, flicked ends, overshooting corners; quick hatching and zigzags for shadow, on figures and ground. Bold transparent washes with soft wet edges, blooms and uneven pigment (not flat polygons) cover about 70% of the frame, shifted 2–4 px (at 1920×1080) off the line. Colours: olive #70783E, ochre #CEA054, smoke grey #706C70, one hot accent #F0822E for sky and light sources; skin in diluted ochre; sketchbook paper #F2ECDE. Bare paper only in highlights, a thin edge band and brush gaps; light grain. A scrawled pencil note (place, time, weather) in a corner, 1/40 of frame height, in the user's language. Layer for lateral tracks; hard cuts like turning a page. Reference: US Army Vietnam Combat Artists Program (1966–70). Avoid: polished finish; empty page; muddy washes; heavy noise.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. jungle | 0.8 seconds

Lateral track along a patrol pushing through elephant grass; Clawd, in a boonie hat and dog tags, stands among them as the soldiers file past.

#### 2. heli | 0.8 seconds

Hard cut to the view down from the Huey's door: the burning landing zone, rotor-flattened grass and the helicopter's own shadow, Clawd tiny in the clearing; a slow pull-out.

#### 3. still | 0.9 seconds

Hard cut close on Clawd, pulling back to the poster framing: arms flung up in a V under a napalm-orange sky, soldiers running past on both sides as the helicopter lifts away.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays Elias: a boonie hat and dog tags. Two hard cuts go from the ground to the air and back, each shot like a different sketchbook page; right after the last cut the arms fling up into the poster pose and sag a little as the helicopter climbs out. The scene runs at 1.1×.
