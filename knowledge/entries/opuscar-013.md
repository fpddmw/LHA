# 013 | Rebecca: Gothic Charcoal Drawing

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

Charcoal tone, erasure, and smudging produce a dream manor, fog, and fire, while repeated inward moves tighten the view.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Paper tooth appears in midtones, deep blacks stay dense, and erasure produces fog and edge light. Material operations support the Gothic dream: the manor emerges through the image's value structure.

The material account offers several ways of making a light area: leaving paper open, lifting charcoal from a toned surface, or separating it from a dense black mass. These produce different edges and textures. A new charcoal treatment can choose among them according to the scene's illumination and desired atmosphere, making the location of tone and erasure part of the visual proposal.

The shots approach the drive, the figure, the burning manor, and Danvers at a window, each moving inward. Charcoal handling and subjective viewing create suspense together. Fire remains monochrome in this design, allowing comparison with schemes that depend on warm firelight.

### Original author's design explanation

Charcoal on toothed paper: velvet blacks, smudged greys, moonlight and mist lifted out with an eraser. Hitchcock's Rebecca is a black-and-white Gothic dream of a house in mist and then in flames, and charcoal can draw both.

### Complete original style prompt

Charcoal on toothed paper. Fine tooth (grain ~1/600 of frame width): light pressure catches only the peaks, heavy pressure fills the valleys. Grain only in midtones; velvet blacks, clean-paper lifts. Values: ~60% dark-to-mid grey, 15% deep black in the nearest foreground and largest masses, lights small. Charcoal #121111 on paper #EEECE7. Marks: broken vine lines, side-of-the-stick sweeps (1/60-1/30 of frame width) following form, hatching, finger smudges, eraser lifts for edges and glows; mist is horizontal streaks erased out of a toned area (as in William Kentridge's animated drawings). Only the hero the story centres on is in pastel (e.g. #D97757), laid fairly solid, tooth peeking through, outlined in charcoal; flames stay charcoal-and-paper. Moonlight from above; 2-3 fog bands drift at different speeds. Camera: slow subjective push, foreground parting. Avoid: digital noise, smooth digital glows, grey blacks, empty bright skies.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. drive | 0.9 seconds

Subjective push up the overgrown moonlit drive; the trees part on Manderley in the mist, its phantom windows glowing.

#### 2. close | 0.6 seconds

Close on Clawd as the second Mrs de Winter, in a velvet headband and cardigan collar, Manderley behind; a slow push in.

#### 3. house | 0.5 seconds

Manderley in moonlight, the camera pushing in as fire breaks out across its windows.

#### 4. danvers | 0.5 seconds

Mrs Danvers standing motionless in the burning window, hands folded, her cold face lit flat; a push in toward her face.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays the second Mrs de Winter in a velvet headband and cardigan, looking up at the house. It stays out of the opening dream push; then three hard cuts go Clawd → the house catching fire → Danvers, and every shot pushes in, so the 2.5 s keep moving toward the fire.
