# 079 | The Departed: Street Stencil on Boston Brick

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

Street stenciling organizes a phone, a rat, and slogans as narrative clues on one wall.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Stencil bridges, spray edges, paint entering mortar, and pasted posters connect image to supporting wall. The figures are themselves a painted layer, maintaining the setting's media boundary.

After the work appears in layers, phone, gaze, and rat shots develop the theme. The case supports study of how symbols and scene clues explain each other, while the urban story remains this proposal's choice.

### Ingredients

- Multi-layer spray stencil
- Banksy's rats
- Red-brick wall texture
- Halftone paste-up poster

### Original author's design explanation

Flat spray-paint stencil layers with overspray and drips, the paint sinking into brick mortar, beside a torn paste-up poster. The Departed ends on a rat crossing a railing below the State House dome, and street stencils have their own tradition of rats.

### Complete original style prompt

Street stencil graffiti on an old red-brick wall. Each piece is 3–5 stencils sprayed as flat colour, photo-derived in look (smallest cut detail about 1/200 of frame width): hard cut edges, soft overspray where a stencil lifted, spray dots, drips, stencil bridges. Palette: black #151312, mid grey #8C8A85, gold #C99A2E, red #C3261F; brick #7A3224, cream painted patches under the main pieces. Brick relief, mortar and rain streaks show through, so paint sinks into the joints. A busy wall: several pieces side by side, short hand-sprayed slogans about the subject (user's language), old tags, a torn halftone paste-up. Light: soft raking daylight, mild contrast, a faint swaying wire shadow; no heavy vignette. Camera: pieces spray on layer by layer, then close-ups and lateral tracks. Reference: Banksy. Avoid: clean vector edges; paint floating on the brick; too many colours; a dark wall.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. wall | 0.8 seconds

Wide of the whole wall: the stencil sprays itself on layer by layer (city, gold dome, railing, rat, Clawd with the phone, red tagline); a slight push in.

#### 2. phone | 0.6 seconds

Close on the two sprayed flip phones ringing across the gap, with Clawd's own phone at its head; Clawd listens, eyes sliding left; a slow drift right.

#### 3. rat | 0.6 seconds

Medium shot of the rat scurrying along the railing under the gold dome, 'WHO'S THE RAT?' sprayed at upper left, a siren washing the brick; the camera tracks right with it.

#### 4. close | 0.4 seconds

Close on Clawd: it snaps the phone down and looks right; a slight push in.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays an undercover cop with a flip phone pressed to its head, sprayed as one layer of the wall. The piece paints itself in over the first two beats; then the phones, the rat and Clawd hanging up follow in fast cuts. Nobody speaks; the ringing and the rat tell it.

### References named by the author

Martin Scorsese, 2006 (the final shot) · Michael Ballhaus (cinematography) · The Departed 2006 poster · Banksy
