## Editorial reading and design relationships

The source distinguishes stones arranged along contours from rows across planes. Grout, cut-edge shadows, and gold reflections give the image a material logic that differs from a regular pixel grid.

A mosaic proposal can describe how rows turn around forms and how grout and cut edges respond to light. That construction explains an appearance different from a fixed pixel grid. Tile size, stone palette, and animated glints can then be chosen for the scale and mood of the new subject.

A descending thumb, an arena figure, and an Elysian wheat field change location and mood in sequence. The material persists while meaning changes, showing how one medium can support several passages.

### Original author's design explanation

Everything is laid in square stone and glass tesserae: courses follow the outlines, flat areas run in straight rows, gold-leaf tiles glint. The film's world is Rome, so the arena and Elysium are built as a Roman floor mosaic.

### Complete original style prompt

Roman floor mosaic: over a flat underdrawing lay square tesserae about 1/150 of frame width: two to three courses follow each outline (opus vermiculatum), flat areas in straight rows, figures outlined with one course of dark stones. Muted, warm stone and glass: burnt gold #E2B24C, umber #5B4332, terracotta #8E3C2B, warm marble #E9DDC4, with imperial-purple glass for one accent only; sky and water in pale grey-blue and cream stone, never saturated bands. Tesserae slightly irregular, lit from the upper left, shadowing into dark 1–2 px grout; gold-leaf glass glints slowly on light sources. Frontal, slightly stiff Pompeian figures before a faded field of fine stones. Short pushes and pulls; motion from details (a gesture, a crowd, plants, a light shaft). References: *Alexander Mosaic*, Zliten mosaic. Avoid: a neat grid that reads as pixel art; oversized tiles; smooth gradients; bright grout.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. emperor | 0.8 seconds

Close on the purple imperial box: Commodus's thumb turns from up to down; a slight push in.

#### 2. still | 1.0 seconds

Poster-style wide of the arena: Clawd as Maximus in the centre with the gladius held low, the chained tiger at left, Tigris at right, the crowd rising; a slight pull back.

#### 3. farm | 0.8 seconds

Wide of Elysium: Clawd stands in golden wheat under a low sun, with cypresses and the farmhouse on the horizon; a slow push in.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays Maximus: one bronze pauldron and a Roman gladius. The thumb turning down opens the film; a hard cut to the arena lets the crowd rise around Clawd; the last cut leaves the fight for the quiet wheat of Elysium. Three hard cuts of about a second each.
