# 022 | All the King's Men: Wood-Type Campaign Poster

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

A campaign poster organizes political performance through wood type, a portrait, and a crowd's response.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Red, blue, and black plates, wood-grain ink losses, a halftone portrait, and heavy figure outlines establish propaganda imagery. Headline, slogan, and figure respectively carry candidate identity, collective speech, and the act of addressing an audience.

A fist rises in the wide view, the crowd responds, and a close-up punch completes the rhythm. Camera vibration follows the speech gesture. The newspaper case offers a comparison between circulation and mobilization through related text media.

### Original author's design explanation

A Southern campaign poster in wood-type letterpress: red, blue and black ink on cream stock, wood grain in the solids, starved patches, misregistered formes and halftone portraits. It is Willie Stark's rally; Clawd is printed in a fourth, orange ink.

### Complete original style prompt

A letterpress poster from wood type and cuts. Three inks on cream stock #EEE3C7: blue #20488C (sky, water, shadow masses), red #C82C28 (warm surfaces, stripes), black #1E1B1E (outlines, type); only the hero the story centres on gets a spot ink (e.g. #D97757). Each ink 2-4 px off register; solids knock out the inks beneath, halftones overprint. Wood grain, pinholed starved patches, squeezed edges. Mid tones: coarse dots (about 1/200 of frame width), varied angles, on at most a third of the frame. Figures as linocuts: heavy outlines, gouged white lines, crescent shadows. Picture fills about 70%; short wood-type lines (about 1/10 of frame height, user's language) with offset shadows sit on banners and signs. Camera: small pushes, a lateral track, a bump on each strong beat (or every 2 s). Reference: Hatch Show Print. Avoid: busy grey mid-tones, perfect registration.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. rally | 0.9 seconds

Wide on the bunting-hung platform in the town square: Clawd as Stark in front of its own giant halftone portrait, fist half up, the crowd roaring under STARK FOR GOVERNOR placards; small push-in.

#### 2. crowd | 0.7 seconds

Into the crowd: placards pumping, fists and shouting mouths, EVERY MAN A KING; lateral track right.

#### 3. close | 0.9 seconds

Close on Clawd, fedora pushed back and tie loosened: the fist punches all the way up and pumps again; the camera bumps on the punch.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays Willie Stark: a pushed-back fedora, a loosened red-and-blue tie, a rolled speech in one hand. The fist comes half up in the wide, the crowd answers in the middle shot, and in the close-up it punches all the way up on the beat and pumps once more, with a small camera kick on the hit. Hard cuts throughout.
