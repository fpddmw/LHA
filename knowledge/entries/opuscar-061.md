# 061 | Rain Man: 80s Airbrush + Raymond's Count

Airbrush illustration and counting annotations share an image, with the annotation layer expressing how a character understands the world.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Soft gradients inside hard masks, simplified figures, and chrome highlights establish an illustration context from the 1980s. Cyan leaders, grouping circles, and monospaced numbers supply another information layer, organized around a specific act of counting.

Grouped toothpicks, a reaction, and card-table totals unfold in sequence. Annotations change with objects and events. The case helps consider when an interface or diagram expresses cognition and when it is mainly decoration.

### Original author's design explanation

Smooth airbrushed gradients, hard masked edges, chrome glints and pink-and-mint neon, overlaid with thin cyan callouts that count. It pairs the film's 1980s look with what Raymond sees: 82, 82, 82, 246 toothpicks, then cards counted at the table.

### Complete original style prompt

1980s airbrush illustration with a drafting overlay. Every shape: a hard-edged mask filled with a smooth sprayed gradient, 1–2 px overspray rim (at 1920×1080). Bright, high-key: mint #79BDA6 walls or sky, cream, neon pink #F58CB5 and magenta #C9467F accents, black-and-white checker floor or ground; purple and night under 20%; at other times of day keep mint and cream; pink carries any sunset. Figures à la Patrick Nagel (flat pale skin, few crisp lines, dark hair masses, pink and mint rim light), keeping their own age and dress. Metal and glass in Hajime Sorayama chrome: banded sky reflection, hard horizon line, cross-star glints. Overlay: thin cyan callouts #56E6FF, near-white text #D9FBFF (1/40 frame height), dotted leaders, dashed rings, dimension lines; monospace numbers count whatever the story counts, Rain Man style, as each group is circled. Slow push/pull. Avoid: synthwave suns; UI boxes; soft edges.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. toothpicks | 1.0 seconds

Overhead of the checkered diner floor: the spilled toothpicks are circled pile by pile as the count rolls up, with a slow pull-back to fit the 246 across the top.

#### 2. raymond | 0.8 seconds

Hard cut to a medium close-up of Clawd in a plaid collar with a pocket of pens, its head tilting further over the piles and their 82 callouts; a slow pull-out.

#### 3. cards | 0.7 seconds

Hard cut over the dealer's shoulder at a Vegas blackjack table: cards arc out of the shoe, and Hi-Lo tags and a running count tick up beside them; Clawd sits between the Nagel woman and Charlie.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays Raymond: a buttoned-up plaid collar and a breast pocket of pens and a pencil. The count drives the edit: three piles circled in the opening beats, the head tilt as 246 appears, then a hard cut to Vegas where every card lands with its tag. The scene runs at 1.1×.
