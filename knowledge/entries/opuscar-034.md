# 034 | West Side Story: Technicolor 3D Stage Set

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

A musical street retains theatrical color within 3D space, while finger snaps, jumps, and matching cuts establish rhythm.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Simplified geometry and dense surface detail coexist. Warm sun, cool fill, and a painted sunset turn the street into a performance space. Puppet-like figures, fire escapes, a court, and rooftops make the subject recognizable.

An overhead descent, a move along the formation, and a rise with a jump depend on performance. The opening inherits a desk-grid pattern; a closing match connects to the next sunrise. Local style therefore needs to be read within the segment and its neighboring transitions.

### Original author's design explanation

A saturated, stage-like 3D block of the Upper West Side under a red-orange Technicolor sunset: red brick, zigzag fire escapes, rooftop water towers, posable dancers. It restages the aerial opening, the Jets' snaps and the poster's leap against the red sky.

### Complete original style prompt

Technicolor 3D stage set, like a 1960s studio musical. Simplified geometry, dense detail on every built surface (openings with sills, ledges, railings, ladders, strung lines); textured brick, plaster or rock, never plain. Theatrical light: a low warm sun rakes surfaces with long shadows; cool fill a quarter of the key. Painted sky of red-orange #E0481E, gold-edged cloud bands, violet only at the top edge; warm haze in the distance. The one hero subject in saturated orange #D97757, at least 1/6 of frame height, set against dark brick or given a black rim so it never melts into the sky. Puppet-like mannequin figures. Warm, saturated grade, fine grain (±3%). Signature shot: a low angle against the orange sky; then one continuous descent into the set and a ground-level push. Avoid: photoreal grime, violet-pink skies, plain walls, poses that pop.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. aerial | 1.3 seconds

Straight-down aerial over the West Side grid (rooftops, water towers, the playground court), descending and tilting up into the street.

#### 2. street | 1.7 seconds

Low at the court's edge, the Jets' wedge snapping fingers behind Clawd by the graffiti wall; one continuous push swings round and in to a close-up of Clawd snapping on the drum hits.

#### 3. leap | 1.2 seconds

Low angle under the fire escape: Clawd crouches and leaps in one arc against the red sky beside a dancer's jeté, the camera tilting up with the arc.

#### 4. escape | 0.8 seconds

Close on the fire-escape landing: Clawd strikes a match, the camera pushes in until the flame sits at frame centre, then it is blown out.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd joins the Jets with no costume; the set does the identifying. Snaps land on the drum hits, and the leap is one clean arc from crouch to landing squash. The opening aerial answers The Apartment's desk grid, and the struck match sits at frame centre exactly where Lawrence of Arabia's sun rises after the cut.
