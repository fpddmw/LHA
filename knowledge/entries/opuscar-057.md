# 057 | Amadeus: Rococo Silhouettes in Gilt Candlelight

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

Silhouettes, an opera house, and a watcher in darkness give one performance viewpoints on and off the stage.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Hairstyles, profiles, cutouts in clothing, and gold edges differentiate dark figures. Gold boxes and red velvet create an outer frame. A few faces remain visible, making the distribution of character information a design choice.

The camera rises from pit to stage, then cuts to Salieri watching from darkness. Performer and observer change the meaning of the same musical moment. Their relationship supplies compositional and narrative knowledge beyond the palette alone.

### Original author's design explanation

Black paper profiles with gold-bronzed rims fill a horseshoe of gilt boxes and red velvet facing a starry stage. Amadeus peaks with Mozart's opening night, so one frame holds the pit, the stage, the audience and Salieri brooding in his box.

### Complete original style prompt

18th-century cut-paper silhouettes. Each figure: a solid black profile #0B0705 with its own cut detail (hair, nose, lace holes, props), a 2–3 px pale-gold rim #F7E3A0 (at 1920×1080) toward the nearest flame, and hairline gold lines along clothing folds. Gilt relief #B88A2E and red velvet #6E0F14 frame the scene like an opera box; an outdoor subject becomes a lit painted backdrop on a stage, cut-paper figures in front. Reds and golds fill most of the frame; night blue #0C1A3A only for the far sky. Only the hero gets a painted, coloured face. Candles flicker gently (±5%), warm halos about five flames wide; light haze, vignette, fine grain. Signature shot: silhouettes framing a bright stage far back; slow lateral glide, then push. References: silhouettists Edouart, Miers; cinematographer Miroslav Ondříček (*Amadeus*, 1984). Avoid: plain black blobs; copy-pasted profiles; blue walls; strobing flames.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. pit | 1.6 seconds

Tracks along the candlelit pit onto Clawd conducting with its baton, then cranes up and back over it to the starry stage as the Queen of the Night raises her arms for the high note.

#### 2. box | 0.9 seconds

Hard cut to Salieri's dark box: among the silhouettes, his is the only painted face, under-lit by a single candle; a slow push-in. Clawd is off-screen.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays Mozart: a powdered wig with a pink-ribboned queue and a lace jabot, the baton beating time with the music. One camera move rises from the pit to the stage to land on the Queen's high note, then a hard cut to Salieri watching in the dark. Real time, no slow motion.
