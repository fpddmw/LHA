# 029 | Around the World in 80 Days: 1950s UPA Modernist Cartoon

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

Limited animation compresses travel into maps, landscapes, and character gags, using geometric forms and shallow parallax for rapid changes of place.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Offset line and color, crayon grain, cutout-like forms, and large pale areas connect the treatment to the cited modernist title animation. A spinning globe and red route first establish world travel, followed by a balloon and character action.

Flat facets, offset outlines, and shallow parallax explain the cartoon's graphic space. A new proposal can develop those relationships through the scene's large shapes and smaller figures. The period palette and rapid cutting rhythm are additional choices; the medium can be considered independently of that particular journey structure.

Map, wide view, and close-up distribute information at different scales. Simplification supports that distribution. Comparison with rubber-hose animation reveals different ways of organizing action, form, and recognition of place.

### Original author's design explanation

A flat, geometric 1950s cartoon: colour blocks printed off their ink lines, broken outlines, crayon grain on cream paper. The film's own six-minute end-title animation was made by Saul Bass in this modernist style.

### Complete original style prompt

1950s UPA modernist cartoon. Uneven ink lines #221C19 about 1/400 of frame width, sometimes broken; colour blocks sit about 1/300 of frame width off them, all one way. Objects are overlapping flat cut-out facets: a lit face and a shadow face one flat step (about 15%) darker. Pale aqua sky and cream paper #F2E9D3 cover most of the frame; mustard #E3A92B, teal #2B8C86, orange-red #D4452B are accents; sky, greens and slate blues are these mixed toward paper. Other times of day change only the sky colour. Crayon grain ±5% brightness everywhere, few flecks. Poster layout: one big graphic shape (a building or sun disc), angular outlined figures beside it. Light beams: flat pale wedges. Hard cuts every 1–2 s; slow parallax drift. After UPA, *Gerald McBoing-Boing* (1950); Saul Bass, *Around the World in 80 Days* titles (1956). Avoid: gradients, perfect registration, saturated colour everywhere.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. globe | 0.9 seconds

Map insert, locked off: a globe turns on a teal ground as the red route runs London, Suez, Bombay, Yokohama, San Francisco; a small balloon carrying Clawd floats at the right.

#### 2. balloon | 0.8 seconds

Wide: the mustard-and-blue checkerboard balloon drifts over the faceted Alps and a Swiss village, Clawd in a top hat at the basket rail; slow lateral pan.

#### 3. close | 0.8 seconds

Close on Clawd as Fogg, with top hat, furled umbrella and a raised champagne coupe, while Passepartout leans out with an ice bucket to scoop snow off the peak; slight push in.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays Phileas Fogg: top hat, furled umbrella, a glass of champagne. Three hard cuts, two beats each: map, wide, close. The route races round the globe, the balloon drifts over the Alps, and the close lands on Passepartout scooping snow to chill the champagne.
