# 016 | Casablanca: Studio Glamour Black-and-White

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

Studio-monochrome facial modeling and two long camera moves connect relationships in a cafe with a farewell in fog.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

The account combines airbrushed forms, short lighting on faces, catchlights, striped shadows, and localized smoke. Light selectively reveals faces and surroundings across different spaces.

A continuous viewpoint in the cafe establishes relationships among several people. The sole hard cut moves to the airport, followed by a retreat into fog. Turning heads, a raised glass, and camera movement share the expressive work. Lighting positions and color emphasis remain traceable choices within this design.

### Original author's design explanation

The film's own look, Arthur Edeson's studio black-and-white, painted as a retouched glamour still: rich greys, soft-focus close-ups with catchlights, venetian-blind shadows, light shafts in smoke and fog. Clawd, as Rick, is the only colour.

### Complete original style prompt

1940s Hollywood studio black-and-white, painted like a retouched glamour still. Forms: smooth airbrushed tone blocks. Faces: short key (light on the face's far side from camera; the near cheek in shadow, holding a Rembrandt triangle of light), two catchlights per eye. Slatted shadows from anything at hand (blinds, railings, branches) fall diagonally across surfaces; light shafts show only in smoke or fog. Values: 30-40% of the frame deep black, whites small and glowing; outdoor skies mid-dark. Grade: diffusion glow, an S-curve, fine grain (±3%), soft vignette, faint flicker; fog stays local. Only the hero the story centres on keeps colour, on its body or costume (e.g. #D97757); skin and lamps stay grey. Camera: a long glide, a push to a face, a pull-back into fog. References: cinematographer Arthur Edeson (*Casablanca*, Michael Curtiz, 1942). Avoid: flat lighting, grey haze, stiff busts.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. cafe | 2.5 seconds

One travelling move through Rick's Café: past fans and smoke to Sam at the piano with Ilsa leaning on it, a push toward her soft-focus face as the tear rises, then right across the room to Clawd at its table in a bow tie, raising the champagne glass.

#### 2. airport | 2.5 seconds

Hard cut to the foggy airfield: close on Clawd under the fedora with its collar up, a glance back, then a long pull-back to Ilsa, Renault and the Electra with its propellers turning in the fog.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays Rick: bow tie and champagne coupe in the café, fedora and turned-up trench collar at the airport. The café is one unbroken camera move ending on its toast, 'Here's looking at you, kid'; the single hard cut at the halfway beat jumps to the airport, where a glance back and a long pull-back close the scene.
