# 089 | Moonlight: Moonlight-Blue Oil Portraiture

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

Color divisions in oil portraiture join support in water, a composite face, and a beach pair to organize identity and relationships.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Low-contrast strokes remain on smooth volumes, keeping faces readable. Teal, magenta, and blue-violet distinguish light and image sections, linked in the source to the film's poster and subject.

A half-submerged view reveals supporting hands; three portraits are then combined before two figures sit together. Bodily relationships, image combination, and quiet duration share the expressive work. These are specific narrative choices within the painting treatment.

### Original author's design explanation

Painted oil portraiture in saturated teal, magenta and moon-blue, with sculpted heads repainted in form-following strokes. It takes the film's title line, "in moonlight black boys look blue", literally.

### Complete original style prompt

Night oil portraiture: smooth volumes repainted in two passes of short, low-contrast (±8% value) form-following strokes: broad (1/60 of frame width) on water, sky and cloth, fine (1/150) on faces, which stay smooth. Palette by role: sky, sea and far land mid blue-violet #32377a, about 60% of the frame; moonlit planes and sand pale lavender-blue; teal #128a91 in water and lit sides; magenta #7a2f86 rim; deepest shadows #07071c. Any skin takes blue top planes and a wet highlight, eyes open and lit. Default night: one key, a full moon with a broken glitter path on water; magenta only as a soft rim from a distant glow; other small light sources stay cyan-white points. Grain ±3%. Signature shot: the figure calm, ~2/3 of frame height. Slow push-ins. Reference: Barry Jenkins, *Moonlight* (2016). Avoid: coarse dabs, small dark statue eyes, a murky frame.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. swim | 0.8 seconds

Split-level shot on the waterline of a turquoise Miami sea: a man stands chest-deep and holds Clawd up on his palm, his hand visible under the surface; slow push-in.

#### 2. chapters | 0.8 seconds

Cut to three painted portraits side by side in teal, magenta and violet; they slide into a single wedge collage as the camera reframes, with Clawd standing in the teal panel.

#### 3. beach | 0.9 seconds

Cut to the moonlit beach: two-shot of Kevin and Clawd sitting on the blue sand, full moon upper right, surf breaking; slow push-in.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays Little with no costume: held on the waterline in the swimming lesson, standing among the three faces, then sitting on the sand beside Kevin. Three chapters in three hard cuts; the sea and beach shots push in slowly so the stillness carries.
