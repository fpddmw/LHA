# 027 | On the Waterfront: Ben Shahn Social Realism

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

Social-realist drawing and the juxtaposition of dock and rooftop jointly portray the character's situation.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Trembling open ink lines, offset tempera shapes, dragged dry brush, large hands, and long faces define this proposal. The source explicitly relates the artist's working-class subjects to the film, giving its figure treatment a specific context.

Hands grabbing work tags and hands holding a pigeon carry different meanings in different places. Noisy hiring and a quiet rooftop show how a shared body part gains expression through action and relationships.

### Original author's design explanation

Ben Shahn's social realism: a trembling, blotted ink line over flat tempera blocks laid slightly off the drawing, big hands and long working faces, restrained slate, brick and ochre. Shahn painted dockers and union halls, which is this film's world.

### Complete original style prompt

Ben Shahn's social-realist tempera. Ink lines (about 1/500 of frame width) jitter and wander, break each outline into two or three strokes, sometimes doubled by a fainter offset ghost (a blotted line). Flat tempera blocks sit 2-4 px off the line, rough-edged, dry-brush streaks (one brush wide, about 1/100 of frame width) over a third of each block; model form with a darker flat of the same hue. Palette by role: slate #768492 sky and cool areas, brick #9E4834 and ochre #C89852 ground, buildings, clothes, ink #1F1A1A, warm board #F0E9DA; skin pale ochre; other colours: these darkened or greyed. The hero the story centres on wears the one warm accent on its main garment (e.g. #D97757). Big heads and hands, long faces, heavy jaws, heavy clothes. Camera: short pushes, tilt up, close-up. Avoid: smooth gradients, clean closed outlines, cheerful colour.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. hands | 0.8 seconds

Medium on the morning shape-up at Pier B: dockers jam the gate and their hands go up for the brass tags, Clawd out of frame; slight push-in.

#### 2. still | 1.0 seconds

Wide on Terry's rooftop coop: Clawd, in a knit cap and plaid collar, holds a pigeon on its raised hand beside Edie, the water tower and Manhattan skyline behind, as the flock lifts off; slow push with a slight tilt up.

#### 3. close | 0.8 seconds

Close on Clawd and the grey pigeon on its hand, a freighter, rooftops and the skyline over its shoulder; slight push-in.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays Terry Malloy: a navy knit cap, red-and-black plaid collar and cuff, a pigeon on its hand. The hard cut from the clamouring hands at the dock to the quiet rooftop puts the film's two worlds side by side; the flock rises across the middle shot, and the close-up rests on Clawd and the bird. Runs 1.8× fast.
