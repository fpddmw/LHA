# 067 | Forrest Gump: Sabuda Pop-up Book on a Real Desk

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

Physical page turns perform the editing in a pop-up book, giving several life episodes one visible storytelling apparatus.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Flat cards, folds, shadows between layers, and a real desk establish the paper space. The source distinguishes room illumination from day and night printed on cards, allowing viewing environment and story environment to differ.

Paper depth is expressed through folds, exposed card edges, shadows between layers, and focus. Those features can inform a physical-paper interpretation of a new scene. A model would additionally decide whether the book and desk belong in the proposed world and whether turning a page serves its events; a request for book illustration alone leaves those choices open.

A feather leads the camera into the book, and two page turns connect bench, road, and shrimp boat. One continuous camera move contains narrative jumps: continuity of photography and continuity of story time are separate properties.

### Ingredients

- Watercolour and coloured-pencil print
- three.js paper cards
- Physical depth of field

### Original author's design explanation

Printed cards standing up in parallel folds, with white cores, grey board edges, layered shadows and shallow focus, on a walnut desk. Forrest tells his life on a bench, so each page that pops up is one chapter.

### Complete original style prompt

A real pop-up book filmed on a desk. Every element: a flat die-cut card on parallel folds before a 90° backdrop page, printed with transparent watercolour and grainy coloured-pencil hatching; cut edges show a white core and grey board. Default light: bright window daylight from the upper left, soft shadows between layers. Print colours (sky blue, warm sand, greens) and room light stay daylight whatever the story's time of day; that time appears only in the print (e.g. a low sun, a night sky). Walnut desk #6B3E1E; the room a warm blur. Physical depth of field, focus on the hero's layer. Signature shot: a high three-quarter view of the whole spread. One continuous move: dive into a page, pull back, turn the page instead of cutting, push into the next spread. References: Robert Sabuda, Matthew Reinhart. Avoid: solid-looking cards; dark moody rooms; cartoon faces.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. one | 5.0 seconds

One continuous shot: the closed book on the desk opens and Savannah's square pops up; the camera drops in with a falling white feather to the bench, where Clawd offers the nurse a chocolate; it pulls back, the page turns, and it pushes in to US-163 in Monument Valley, where Clawd in a red cap runs in ahead of the bearded followers, stops and turns to them; it pulls back again, the page turns, and it pushes in to the shrimp boat Jenny in a lightning storm.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays Forrest: a box of chocolates on the bench, a red cap on the road and on the boat. Twelve beats with no cuts: the feather carries the camera in, and two page turns, at about 2.7 s and 4.2 s, stand in for cuts. The run starts at 3.25 s, with the stop and the turn right after.
