The source explicitly bases its material choice on cloth's symbolic role. Block divisions, dye spreading along warp and weft, indigo resist patterns, and profile figures let production technique participate in the visual structure.

A pan along the march, raised salt, and a turning wheel connect collective action, a symbolic object, and production. The relationship concerns a material within the subject becoming a narrative support. Patterns and colors remain specific choices.

### Original author's design explanation

Hand block print on cotton in indigo, madder and turmeric with a carved black key block, misregistered at the block seams, dye bleeding along the weave. Homespun khadi was Gandhi's symbol, so the Salt March is printed as cloth.

### Complete original style prompt

Hand block print on cotton, Rajasthan style (Bagru, Sanganer). Undyed cotton #EEE5CF is the ground, over half the frame, and the skin colour; indigo #2A3A68 for water or sky bands and patterns; madder red #AA322A for borders, clothing and the hero; turmeric #DEA42C for small motifs; black key block #281E1C for outlines. Stamp blocks about 1/8 of frame height, misregistered 2–4 px, tone jumping slightly at seams even in large bands; dye bleeds along the weave, fills unevenly. Mud-resist crackle only inside indigo, fine and sparse. Carved, rough outlines about 1/300 of frame width. Flat frieze in a paisley and vine border; waves as scallop rows; profile figures with miniature-painting faces, kohl-lined eyes. Camera: pan along the frieze, push in; cut on a turning motif. Avoid: vector edges, perfect registration, faceless figures, dense crackle, indigo flooding the frame.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. crowd | 0.8 seconds

A lateral pan along the procession on the shore: volunteers in white khadi, women in indigo and madder saris, the Swaraj flag; Clawd comes into frame at the head of the line.

#### 2. salt | 1.1 seconds

Medium close-up, a slow push: Clawd as Gandhi in round spectacles and a khadi shawl stoops, scoops a handful of salt, raises it glinting, then turns to the marchers and lifts it higher.

#### 3. wheel | 0.6 seconds

A village woman spinning at her charkha; the camera pushes in on the turning wheel for the cut to the next film.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays Gandhi: round wire spectacles, the edge of a khadi shawl, a bamboo staff, a handful of salt. The scene runs at 1.1× with no slow motion. A hard cut from the march to the gesture: stoop, scoop, raise, then turn to the marchers and lift it higher. The charkha's turning wheel ends the film as a spin transition.
