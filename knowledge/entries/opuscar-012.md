# 012 | Gone with the Wind: Technicolor Impasto Oil

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

Impasto, Technicolor color, and epic viewing scales connect a personal vow to large historical scenes.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Palette-knife strokes spread across the sky, short strokes follow figures and terrain, and thin paint contrasts with raised highlight ridges. These surface relationships join a gold-red sky, low horizon, and backlit silhouettes. The epic effect depends on both material surface and composition.

Tara, the vow, fire, and a rising view over wounded soldiers repeatedly shift between personal and historical scale. The color reveal also participates in the anthology's transition from monochrome to color. That context matters when considering which parts of the scheme to transfer.

### Original author's design explanation

Impasto oil with palette-knife slabs, ridged paint and Technicolor heat: skies burning from gold to blood red, red earth, black silhouettes. Gone with the Wind is where colour arrives in the film, so Tara at sunset is the first fully saturated frame.

### Complete original style prompt

Impasto oil in a 1939 Technicolor palette. Palette-knife slabs: long horizontal sky bands (1/15-1/6 of frame width long, about 1/60 tall), shorter strokes following form on land and figures, calm thin areas between; ridges lit from upper left, paint thickest in highlights, canvas weave where thin. Default sunset sky: wine #3A0912, red #A8241A, orange #F27A22, gold #FFD878; ground red-brown #6E2214; warm near-black silhouettes. The hero the story centres on stays a readable warm mid-tone (e.g. #D97757), never a silhouette. Strong backlight, gold rims; in close shots a warm side light on half the face and hands. Epic-poster composition: big dark silhouettes (trees, buildings, rocks, figures) on a low horizon. Lamps as painted flames. Camera: slow push on the wide, low push-in on the hero, crane up to a vista. Avoid: tiled same-size strokes, muddy browns, smooth gradients.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. tara | 1.7 seconds

Tara in silhouette: the great oak, the white house and cedars black against a sky burning from gold toward blood red; Clawd stands on a red-earth rise holding a radish; a slow push in.

#### 2. oath | 1.0 seconds

Low push-in against the burning sky: Clawd in a wide straw hat with a green ribbon raises the radish high, backlit with a gold rim.

#### 3. close | 0.4 seconds

Close on Clawd's face and hat, the ribbon tails whipping in the wind.

#### 4. fire | 1.5 seconds

Atlanta burning: the wagon charges past the flaming warehouse and timber frame, Clawd beside Rhett on the seat, Prissy and Melanie behind; the camera trucks with it as flames sweep the foreground.

#### 5. depot | 2.1 seconds

One crane move at the depot: from Clawd among the wounded, up and back until rows of soldiers cover the red ground to the horizon, a torn flag in the yard and Clawd a single orange dot.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays Scarlett in a wide straw hat with a green ribbon, holding a radish. The sky warms from gold to blood red over the first seven beats and the radish goes up on beat 6. Four hard cuts move through Tara, the oath, Atlanta and the depot, and the last five beats are a single crane shot.
