# Alien biomechanics: a shared logic for organic and artificial structures

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

Living walls, membranes, pulsing light, and biomechanical metal form a whole-world proposal with extensive strategy annotations.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Environment, clothing, and surfaces all grow or respond, while light originates in organs and vein patterns. The design organizes organic and mechanical features as a shared system.

Its coherence comes from organic forms recurring across surfaces and from light emerging within them. A new proposal can explore how much of that relationship belongs in the environment, clothing, or body, with the current request determining the design scope. This makes the combination intelligible beyond a list of veins, organs, and metal.

High-risk labels, preferred carriers, prohibited mixtures, and failure types are the template author's annotations; this entry includes no independent experiment record. They provide context for comparison in a new task. The extent of changes to a person especially depends on task meaning.
