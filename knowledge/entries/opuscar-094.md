# 094 | CODA: Scrimshaw on a Whale Tooth

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

A local craft support connects to fishing life, while lateral movement brings signing and singing together.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Incised hatching, stippling, limited color, and the object's curvature establish the selected craft appearance. The whole object is shown before the view enters its engraved boat and figures, connecting material context and story location.

The camera moves from the father's signs toward singing, with a musical staff extending beside the action. The case explores relationships between modes of expression and their visualization. The original craft and object remain cultural and design references for this work.

### Original author's design explanation

A polished sperm-whale tooth on an oak table, engraved in fine black lines with touches of red, blue and yellow: a Gloucester fishing boat, the family hauling nets, Ruby singing at the bow. It is the folk art of CODA's own New England coast.

### Complete original style prompt

Scrimshaw: 19th-century sailor's engraving on a whale tooth. Signature shot: a broad, flat-sided tooth lies horizontal on an oak table, about 85% of frame width and 70% of its height; ink bottle, jackknife beside. Engrave in near-black #17110c: needle hatching (lines 1/800 of frame width, spaced about 1/500), swelling outlines, stipple. Lines cover ~70% of the face, dense in sea, subject and land, sky lightly hatched; seas as rows of stylised waves. The hero is large (~1/3 of the tooth's height), coloured; otherwise red #9a2a22, blue #2b4a78, yellow #e9b526 only as small touches. Warm ivory, growth bands, scratches, honey tip. Folk layout: rope border, ribbon motto, rayed sun, motifs from the subject's world. Shade the edges, one gloss streak; don't warp the picture. Push in, then pan slowly as waves animate. Avoid: thick lines, solid fills, a slim horn.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. tooth | 0.8 seconds

Wide on the engraved whale tooth lying on an oak table with a jackknife and an ink bottle; the camera pushes in until the tooth fills the frame.

#### 2. boat | 1.7 seconds

Cut into the engraving: medium-wide on the fishing boat, panning slowly left to right with a slight push-in, from Frank signing I-love-you while the net is hauled to Clawd singing at the bow, a stave of notes unrolling from its raised arm.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays Ruby in a yellow sou'wester, standing on a fish crate at the bow. One cut from the object into the picture, then a single gentle pan carries the eye from the father's sign to the song as the stave of notes unrolls.
