### Original author's design explanation

The Trinity test site before dawn rebuilt in real-time 3D, with a fireball of about 720,000 particles, quantum visions of orbits and waves, deep pre-dawn blue against orange-gold fire and IMAX-style grain. Oppenheimer is built on that one light.

### Complete original style prompt

Real-time 3D night lit by one huge particle event: a ~500k-particle fireball (or flare, eruption, wildfire) whose shape follows time: white flash (0.5 s), billowing dome by 1 s, a rolling toroid, a dust wall sweeping out. Right behind the subject like a halo, ~40% of the frame: white-gold core, bright orange-gold billows #ff9a50 with visible texture, black smoke only as thin veins and the top edge (under 15%). Its glow fills everything: warm #ffd8a0 on ground and cloud undersides, a hot rim on the subject, faces lit warm at half strength; long shadows toward camera. The rest is pre-dawn blue #2a3654 and dark earth #16120e. Clip only the core. IMAX grade: grain ±3%, warm halation, vignette, slight gate weave. Low wide angle, subject centred, slow push-ins. Reference: Christopher Nolan, *Oppenheimer* (2023). Avoid: a smooth CG ball, smoke-shrouded fire.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. tower | 0.6 seconds

Low-angle wide on the floodlit steel tower before dawn, the countdown beacon blinking, the camera slowly pushing in.

#### 2. gadget | 0.4 seconds

Cut to the bomb in its corrugated shack: the bolted sphere wrapped in cables under a warm work light; locked off.

#### 3. particles | 0.4 seconds

Cut to the quantum vision on black: glowing orbits round a core, interference waves below, a star collapsing.

#### 4. watch | 0.8 seconds

A white flash, then the reverse shot: observers in hats hold welding glass to their eyes, lit by the fire, with Clawd in the middle in a porkpie hat and pipe; locked off.

#### 5. still | 1.0 seconds

Low-angle wide on Clawd standing in the road, the fireball rising behind it like a halo and embers drifting in the air; slow push-in.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays Oppenheimer with a camel porkpie hat and a briar pipe that smokes. Five hard-cut shots build the countdown: tower, device, vision, then the flash lands on the cut into the watchers, and the music keeps going while the fireball swells behind Clawd for the last second.
