# 085 | Argo: Jack Kirby 70s Comic Page

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Heavy ink, energy bubbles, halftones, dialogue boxes, and spread composition establish comic reading. The source connects this medium directly to the film's fictional production cover story.

Three panels move from space combat to terminal and runway. Figures finally remove their costumes and become passengers. The case explores different levels of reality within one visual language and the correspondence between panels and video shots.

### Original author's design explanation

A 1970s Kirby comic book on a production desk: heavy ink, Kirby Krackle, Ben-Day dots on yellowed newsprint. The film's fake sci-fi movie was sold with real Kirby concept art, so the pages run from fake epic to the real runway escape.

### Complete original style prompt

1970s Jack Kirby comic page. Heavy tapered brush contours, feathered spot blacks, exaggerated perspective, Kirby Krackle energy bubbles, block sound effects, hand-lettered balloons and yellow captions. CMYK flats printed as 45° Ben-Day dots: cyan #1696CD, magenta #DE2C70, yellow #FFD616, black #1C161A on aged newsprint #EEE2C2, colour plates 1 px off register. Paper grain, browned edges, a gutter fold shadow, warm lamp falloff. Lay the story out in panels and move between them: push into the splash, pan along a strip. Dots stay round and grow into pop-art dots on push-ins. Avoid: smooth digital gradients, clean white paper, stiff tube-like machines.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. splash | 0.9 seconds

Medium shot of the cosmic splash panel, slow push-in: Clawd, the caped space hero on a rock ledge, fires its ray gun and the beam blows up the robot's reactor with a KRAKA-THOOM.

#### 2. strip | 0.8 seconds

Hard cut to the red film-strip panel: the houseguests in the terminal and the caption "THE MOVIE WAS FAKE. THE MISSION WAS REAL.", with a slight pan right.

#### 3. runway | 0.8 seconds

Hard cut to the night runway panel in steel blue, panning left to right past the chasing truck and jeeps to the airliner lifting off; Clawd sits behind a porthole in the corner inset, looking back.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays the comic's space hero: a high red-lined collar, blue cape, gold cuffs and a Kirby ray gun. The beam fires inside the first beat, then two hard cuts turn the page from fake epic to real escape, ending with Clawd as a plain passenger at the plane window. Three panels in 2.5 seconds, read like a comic.
