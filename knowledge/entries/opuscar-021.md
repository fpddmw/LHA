# 021 | Hamlet: Deep-Focus Fog Castle, 3D B&W

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

A 3D castle, fog, and waves create a dark space through value and depth, then acquire meaning through a ghost and a soliloquy.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Exposed masonry, wet surfaces, layered fog, and a silver-gray sky establish depth. The source distinguishes a clear middle distance from fogged distant space. Lighting, occlusion, and actual geometry jointly shape this monochrome scene.

The castle view, ghost's appearance, and raised skull each carry a specific relation to the subject. The material supports comparison of how painting and 3D construction produce related dark atmospheres.

### Original author's design explanation

A real-time 3D Elsinore in high-contrast deep-focus black-and-white: volumetric fog, wet stone, swells breaking into lace foam, silver grain. It follows Olivier's castle, foggy battlements and sea rock; Clawd is the only colour, lifting Yorick's skull.

### Complete original style prompt

A real 3D scene in late-1940s deep-focus black-and-white. Built forms: bare masonry, block by block, bevelled, staggered (Romanesque arches only where the setting has them); wet surfaces dark and glossy. Soft overcast light, no hard shadows: dark masses against a silver sky. Low fog fills the lower third; the far background ends at 60-70% grey, the midground sharp; a bright patch behind cloud throws faint shafts. Water, if any: layered swells, lace foam, misty spray. Post: ambient occlusion, gentle bloom, high-contrast silver grade (true blacks, unclipped whites), grain (±3%), faint flicker. Only the hero the story centres on keeps its colour (e.g. #D97757); every light source stays grey. Camera: slow drift in, a track along a high edge, a low angle on the hero. Reference: Laurence Olivier, *Hamlet* (1948). Avoid: hard sunlit shadows, white-out fog, tiled textures.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. wide | 0.7 seconds

Wide from the sea: Elsinore on its headland over the breaking surf, Clawd a tiny figure on the rock at the right; slow drift in.

#### 2. battlements | 0.8 seconds

On the fog-bound wall-walk the armoured Ghost fades in and beckons, Clawd watching from the near side; slight push-in.

#### 3. still | 1.1 seconds

Low angle on the sea rock: Clawd, in lace cuffs with a sheathed short sword, lifts Yorick's skull before the castle in the fog; slow push-in.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays Hamlet: Yorick's skull, the white lace cuffs, belt and short sword from the poster. Three hard cuts walk through the film's three images, the castle on its cliff, the Ghost in the fog and the soliloquy rock, and the skull comes up on the beat in the last shot. Runs 1.8× fast.
