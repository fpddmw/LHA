# Wuxia: action, historical materials, and poetic space

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

A martial-arts film direction built around heroic figures, flowing cloth, and layered landscapes.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Cloth, wood, stone, steel, wind, dust, and mist describe a historical-fantasy space. Silhouettes and moving fabric emphasize action. Treating the characters as martial figures gives the clothing, setting, and prop proposals a narrative assumption.

The text does not develop choreography, distinct wuxia traditions, or specific relationships. Alongside the xianxia card, it allows comparison of these authors' emphases on action and ritual. The distinction describes the available material rather than an exhaustive classification.
