# 068 | Braveheart: Illuminated Manuscript with Gold Leaf

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

An illuminated page combines writing, an initial, battle imagery, and gold leaf; the view passes from letterform into event.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Burnished gold, interlace, Gothic lettering, and window-like miniatures establish the page. A figure occupies a letter, connecting FREEDOM to combat and speech scrolls. Writing is both read and entered as space.

The view moves from initial to whole page, then into bridge and battle. Scale turns layout into action. The legendary subject and mixed references belong to this design; its methods can be compared with other combinations of words and images.

### Original author's design explanation

A medieval manuscript page: burnished gold with punched dots, lapis blue, vermilion, textura script, and a battle miniature framed like a window. Braveheart is a medieval Scottish legend, and its last cry, FREEDOM, becomes the illuminated initial.

### Complete original style prompt

An illuminated manuscript page (cropped, or an open spread) filling the frame. Vellum #ECDCB4, brown ink outlines #2A1A10, lapis #27469A, vermilion #C43A20, burnished gold leaf on raised gesso, with punched dot rosettes; a slow candle glint sweeps the gold only. Miniature backgrounds are mostly gold (night: lapis with gold stars); gold fills at least a tenth of the frame. Gothic figures in flat colour and ink outline. Celtic interlace, ivy borders, marginal grotesques; the story's text (Latin or the user's language) in broad-nib textura, red-and-blue line fillers, one large gilded initial. Layout: the miniature a window about half the page beside the text column, layered for parallax on pushes; figures may break out. Warm candle from upper left, brown vignette, pigment grain. References: Book of Kells, Morgan Bible, Luttrell Psalter. Avoid: flat yellow for gold; modern typefaces; glints on faces.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. initial | 0.7 seconds

Close on the gilded initial F with Wallace inside, half his face in woad, as a glint sweeps across the gold leaf.

#### 2. still | 0.6 seconds

Hard cut to the whole page: REEDOM, the textura text, the margins, and the Stirling Bridge miniature with Clawd leading the charge; a slight push-in.

#### 3. bridge | 0.6 seconds

Hard cut into the miniature: the English crowding over the wooden bridge, a knight in checkered trappers charging, men struggling in the river.

#### 4. close | 0.6 seconds

Hard cut close on Clawd raising its two-handed sword against the knight's lance as the speech scroll unrolls 'freedom' above its head.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays Wallace: a two-handed sword, a tartan sash, a stripe of woad across the brow. Four hard cuts: the initial, the page, the bridge, the clash. The charge builds through the middle, and the 'freedom' scroll unrolls in the last shot. Scene time runs about 1.8× screen time.
