# 092 | Parasite: Architectural Section Axonometric

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

An architectural section makes vertical relationships between spaces jointly readable; a person's route turns them into an experience over time.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Cut surfaces, projection, material fills, and scale figures reveal connections normally hidden by walls. House, bunker, and semi-basement share one terrain, explicitly placing vertical relationships in service of the subject.

A downward route with a stone connects to rain and flooding, followed by a withdrawal to the whole drawing. The case explores social and narrative roles of spatial relationships; particular symbolic interpretations belong to this example.

### Original author's design explanation

One architectural section through a Seoul hillside: the glass house on top, a hidden bunker beneath, stairs down to a flooding half-basement, in fine lines and soft material colours. Parasite is built on that vertical class divide, so one drawing holds the film.

### Complete original style prompt

Vector section axonometric, low dimetric view: the cut face lies along the near-flat 7° axis, facing us, depth along the 42° axis at half scale; roof and near walls cut away to show the rooms. Cut through the subject's place (a house, a tower, a ship, a hillside). Poché on cut faces: dark concrete with aggregate, earth hatched at 45° with stones. 1 px dark-grey lines at every zoom. Default daylight under a pale sky: concrete #b3ada2, oak #d6b58a, charcoal #3e4146, pine #3f6b3e, white walls, green lawn, translucent blue water. Furnished rooms, simple scale figures. Night or other low light is a variation: multiply 30–40% moonlit blue, lit rooms painted back warm. Crane slowly framing 2–3 rooms along one continuous path; end on the whole drawing at ~80% of the frame. Avoid: 30° isometric, thick outlines, cuts between levels, a small drawing.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. still | 0.8 seconds

Daytime section of the glass house and its striped lawn party: Clawd stands in the garden doorway holding the scholar's stone over its head; slow push-in.

#### 2. house | 1.3 seconds

Cut to the stormy night: the Parks' living room with Clawd hiding by the coffee table, then the camera cranes down through the floor slab and the basement to the bunker, where the lights blink Morse.

#### 3. descent | 2.9 seconds

Cut to one continuous descent in the rain: the camera follows Clawd, the stone held over its head, from the gate down the long flights, through a tunnel under the road, into the low town and the flooding half-basement, then cranes out to the whole section, the house on top and the flood at the bottom.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays Ki-woo, carrying the scholar's stone the whole way: over its head on the lawn, crouched by the table in the storm, carrying it down the hill. Two cuts only; inside each shot the camera travels the section vertically so rich and poor share a frame, and the film's 12 beats end on the full drawing.
