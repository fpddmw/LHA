# 049 | Rocky: Philadelphia Sunrise, 3D on 35mm

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

Rapid training cuts become a continuous climb and orbit, revealing the sunrise city as the action reaches completion.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Low warm light, a cool-blue sky, long shadows, mist, and a 3D city allow figure lighting and environmental reveal to develop together. Running speed, following photography, and movement at the summit remain connected.

The training montage accelerates time; the steps restore a more continuous passage. A rising, retreating orbit meets the raised arms. Time and field of view change with the event, while focal lengths and seconds serve this implementation.

### Original author's design explanation

A full 3D Philadelphia at sunrise, from meat locker to museum steps, graded like 1970s 35mm: cold blue sky, low gold sun, long shadows, haze, fine grain. The run up the 72 steps and the rising orbit at the top are what people remember.

### Complete original style prompt

1970s 35mm realism in a detailed 3D world, sunrise by default (sunset the same). Sun 5–10° up, warm orange #F0A060, lighting the hero from the side or three-quarter front so the lit body and long ground shadows read; no full backlight. Cool blue sky #7FA3CF, pink cloud undersides #E6B2AE; haze golden toward the sun, blue-violet away. Grade: fine grain (±3%), cool shadows, warm highlights, nothing clipped; flare and ghosts only with the sun in frame. Camera: a low Steadicam following the hero from behind up an ascent, then at the top one continuous orbit that circles, rises and pulls back over 6–8 s, showing the lit face, until the place spreads out below. References: John G. Avildsen, *Rocky* (1976); Steadicam operator Garrett Brown. Avoid: flat noon light, detail-less silhouettes, a static climax, plastic CG shine.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. meat | 0.4 seconds

Meat locker, medium shot, fixed camera: Clawd in a grey hood jabs at a hanging side of beef among white tiles and bare bulbs.

#### 2. meat_close | 0.4 seconds

Closer, fixed camera: the punches land and the beef swings.

#### 3. street | 0.6 seconds

The Italian Market before dawn, fixed camera: Clawd jogs toward the lens past fire barrels, fruit stands and lit streetlamps.

#### 4. run | 1.5 seconds

A low Steadicam chases Clawd from behind as it runs up the 72 steps toward the museum's columns.

#### 5. orbit | 2.1 seconds

At the top Clawd throws its arms up in a V and hops; the camera circles round it, rising and pulling back until the whole Parkway and the city open up at sunrise.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays Rocky: a grey sweatshirt hood up and a white towel on one shoulder. The training montage runs at about 1.7× over three quick cuts; from the steps on, the scene plays at 1×. A hard cut into the Steadicam run, then no cut: the run flows straight into the orbit, and the victory hop lands as the camera starts to rise.
