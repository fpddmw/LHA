# 031 | Gigi: Lautrec Belle Époque Lithograph

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

Belle Epoque lithography organizes social attention in a restaurant through lateral viewing, with caricature and print marks supporting each other.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Dry brush lines, broad plates, local spatter, and satirical profiles define this lithographic proposal. Cropped foreground figures and shallow parallax create a crowded viewing position. Period character emerges through more than color.

Entering the restaurant, successive guests turning, and a return to the walking pair make being watched a concrete set of relationships. Other gaze-driven cases offer comparisons, while the author's distinction from related period styles such as Mucha remains relevant.

### Original author's design explanation

A Toulouse-Lautrec colour lithograph: flat poster colour, dry-brush contours, spattered ink instead of halftone, caricatured faces. Gigi's big moment is walking into Maxim's while all of Paris stares, and Lautrec drew exactly that Paris.

### Complete original style prompt

Belle Époque colour lithograph after Toulouse-Lautrec. Flat colour plates with wobbly edges; figures and main shapes outlined in a loaded brush (blunt start, swelling middle, split dry tail), about 1/250 of frame width. Crachis only on shadow sides, cast shadows and glows: 1–3 px dots at 1920×1080, sparse, under 15% of the frame, a darker step of the colour beneath. Large surfaces get grouped dry-brush streaks. Palette: yellow #F1C232, orange-red #E0602C, olive #6E7036, black #1D1A16 on cream stone-grained paper #EFE3C3, plus a muted bottle teal and greyed mauve; flesh is orange-red thinned with paper. Caricatured profiles with drawn faces; one large figure cropped by the frame edge in front. Flat, even light; shadows are flat plates. Camera: shallow parallax, a slow pan settling on a medium shot. Avoid: Mucha halos, smooth gradients, regular halftone, all-over speckle.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. maxims | 0.8 seconds

Wide of Maxim's: Clawd (Gaston) in top hat and white tie walks Gigi in on its arm between two cropped foreground silhouettes; the nearest diners start to turn; slow push, drifting right.

#### 2. faces | 0.7 seconds

Lateral pan along the red banquette: caricatured diners turn to stare, lorgnettes up, fans stopped.

#### 3. gigi | 1.0 seconds

Mid shot of the pair still walking in; Gigi's eyes come to the lens and she winks, as on the poster; slight drift right.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays Gaston: top hat, white tie and cane, with Gigi on its arm. The couple keeps walking and the room's gaze follows them. Three cuts on the beat: the entrance, the stares in a pan along the banquette, then the pair again for Gigi's wink to camera.
