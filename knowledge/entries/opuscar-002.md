# 002 | The Broadway Melody: Black-Gold Art Deco Revue Poster

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

An Art Deco musical poster turns the arrival of sound cinema into moving graphic design through microphone waves, bulbs, and chorus formations.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Assigned roles for black, gold, and silver, banded metallic gradients, a stepped proscenium, and elongated figures establish the poster stage. Sound waves from the microphone connect decoration to the arrival of the talkies. Graphic elements carry both period cues and action.

Cuts between signs, stage, microphone, dancers, and close-ups follow the beat, linking the overall formation to a local sound source. The case supports study of temporal organization in flat graphics and of the different roles that metal and spotlights can play. Its palette and dancer configuration belong to this particular proposal.

### Original author's design explanation

An Art Deco revue poster in black, gold and silver: airbrushed gradients, a stepped proscenium, a chrome condenser mic. It is 1929 Broadway at the height of Deco, and the film where sound arrives, so rings of sound pour out of the mic.

### Complete original style prompt

Art Deco revue poster. Black lacquer ground; gold and silver from banded metallic gradients (3-5 hard bands); airbrush glows, sunbursts, ziggurat steps, streamlined curves; a stepped proscenium frame of gold lines and bulbs. Palette: ground #0A0908, gold #D6B560, highlight #F3E2A2, silver #BFC4C9; skin pale silver-cream; one saturated accent (e.g. #D97757) only on the hero the story centres on. Elongated Erté-style figures with real faces; fringe, sequins or feathers where the subject allows. Fine grain, glints, stars. Frontal symmetry, a central spotlight. Motion: chasing bulbs, a row of repeated elements (dancers, waves, columns) rippling toward the centre, rings radiating from the main sound or light source; slow pushes, cut on the music's beat (without music, every 2-3 s). References: A. M. Cassandre's posters, Erté's costumes. Avoid: flat yellow for metal, faceless figures, extra hues.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. marquee | 0.8 seconds

Out of the white: the Astor Theatre marquee, 'THE BROADWAY MELODY / ALL TALKING · ALL SINGING · ALL DANCING', bulbs chasing; Clawd with boater and cane at left; a slow pull back.

#### 2. still | 1.0 seconds

The whole stage: Deco proscenium, chorus girls on the risers, Clawd tapping beside the giant condenser mic as it starts pouring out sound rings; a slow pull back.

#### 3. mic | 0.5 seconds

Close on the chrome spring-ring mic, rings radiating and notes flying; a push in.

#### 4. line | 0.5 seconds

Truck along the kick line: feathered headdresses, sequins, legs kicking in a wave.

#### 5. close | 0.5 seconds

Clawd in close-up, tapping, the boater tilting, the mic ring behind; a slight push in.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd is the hoofer in a straw boater with a black cane, tapping all the way through. The film fades in from No. 1's white; on beat 4 the mic comes alive and the sound rings pour out (the talkies arrive). Four hard cuts run street → stage → mic → chorus line → Clawd.
