### Original author's design explanation

Four universes, each with its own look, all ray-marched in real time: a cold-green IRS office, a hot-dog-finger piano lit like an advert, a silent canyon of googly-eyed rocks, the Everything Bagel as a black hole. A googly eye jumps between them.

### Complete original style prompt

Ray-marched real-time 3D multiverse montage: the subject jumps through 3–4 universes, each its own look, invented from its world, e.g. a realistic workplace under cold green fluorescents, densely dressed; a surreal cosmic version with a gravitational-lens halo; a silent wide landscape with white subtitles; a candy-pink product shot with pink-and-cyan rims. Each holds 2–4 s with its own grade. One link through all: a googly-eye sticker on the subject, about 1/10 of frame height. Transition, 0.5–0.8 s: push into the eye, smear, split RGB, then a rainbow ring of colour segments (#e5322d, #f7c928, #1fa39a, #2a6fc0, #e0478f) studded with googly eyes and pink beads blooms to ~80% of frame height and bursts to white. Signature frame: the ring around the subject; first jump at ~1 s. Reference: Daniels, *Everything Everywhere All at Once* (2022). Avoid: look-alike universes, a small plain ring.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. irs | 0.8 seconds

Wide on the IRS office with receipts flying, the camera dollying down the aisle toward Clawd, who wears a googly eye on its forehead and a bum bag.

#### 2. deirdre | 0.6 seconds

Cut to the auditor at her steel desk piled with receipts, holding one up; slow push-in.

#### 3. eye | 0.4 seconds

Cut to Clawd, arms raised, as the camera pushes hard into the googly eye on its forehead and the verse-jump flash fires.

#### 4. hotdog | 0.5 seconds

The hot-dog-finger universe: wide on a glossy piano under candy-pink light, a hand of floppy hot-dog fingers on the keys, Clawd standing on the keys.

#### 5. hotdog | 0.5 seconds

Cut closer to Clawd's feet stomping the keys, little hot dogs on the ends of its arms.

#### 6. eyeHotdog | 0.4 seconds

Push into the googly eye again, and the next jump.

#### 7. rocks | 1.0 seconds

Wide, still canyon rim: two rocks with googly eyes beside Clawd sitting quietly, subtitle "We're all small and stupid."; the camera drifts slowly along the edge.

#### 8. bagel | 1.7 seconds

The Everything Bagel: Clawd looks up at the giant black bagel, then the camera pushes into its black-hole centre and through the event horizon as Clawd is pulled forward.

#### 9. still | 0.6 seconds

Back in the office: Clawd flings its arms open as a rainbow mandala ring of googly eyes grows behind it; slight pull-back.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays Evelyn with a googly eye on its forehead and a bum bag. Each jump is a push into that eye followed by a smear, RGB split and white flash timed to the cut, then a different action per universe: standing, stomping piano keys, sitting with the rocks, being pulled into the bagel. It ends where it started, arms open in the mandala.
