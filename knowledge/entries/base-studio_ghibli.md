## Editorial reading and design relationships

Matte surfaces, simplified folds, soft color masses, wind, and dust suggest a gentle painterly treatment. The template's concern that people belong naturally in the setting can be read through coherence of contours, materials, and environmental response.

A new proposal can express the selected painterly direction through matte color masses, simplified folds, and environmental movement such as cloth responding to wind.
