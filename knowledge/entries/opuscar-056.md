# 056 | Terms of Endearment: Family Album Polaroids

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

A faded album photograph comes alive; restored color and movement turn an image of the past into a present scene.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

A white border, handwriting, plastic-sleeve reflections, and fading first establish album viewing. Panning blur and a sharp subject arise from motion inside the photograph, distinct from the overhead view outside it.

After the camera enters, car, spray, and people begin to move as color returns. The case offers a transition between a memory object and an active scene. Its added album and event structure would need a task-specific reason in a request concerned only with image appearance.

### Original author's design explanation

An open family album of faded Polaroids and Kodacolor snapshots, with teal-lifted blacks, creamy highlights and white borders, where one Polaroid comes alive as a panning shot of the beach drive. Terms of Endearment spans thirty years of a mother and daughter.

### Complete original style prompt

1970s–80s snapshots in an open album. Faded dyes: blacks lifted to teal grey about #2F3A3A, creamy rolled-off highlights, mild warm cast, slight overexposure, softness, fine grain (±4%), saturation about 60% of normal. White Polaroid borders, photo-booth strips (four frames stacked), ballpoint captions in the user's language. The album fills the frame, cream pages seen from above in soft, even light, a faint plastic sheen. In a photo, a moving subject gets a panning-shutter look (background smeared sideways, subject sharp); still subjects get shallow depth of field. People look photographed: real proportions, soft shading, slight blur, small faces. Camera: an overhead push into one photo until it fills the frame (about 3 s), a cut into the living scene as colour returns, then a pull-back. References: Polaroid SX-70, Kodacolor prints. Avoid: flat cartoon shapes, crisp modern colour, light shafts, saturated prints.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. album | 1.2 seconds

Overhead on the open album on a walnut table, then one push into the loose Galveston Polaroid until its picture fills the frame.

#### 2. beach | 0.8 seconds

The Polaroid comes alive: the red Corvette tears through the surf with Aurora waving in the passenger seat, colour flooding back; the camera pulls back to a wider beach.

#### 3. close | 0.5 seconds

Close on Clawd as Garrett in gold aviators, standing up behind the seats with its arms flung wide; a slow push.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays Garrett, the retired astronaut, in gold aviators, with Aurora laughing beside it. About 1.8× overall. The push into the album becomes a cut into the photo itself, and the faded grade warms back to fresh colour; its arms go up as the colour returns, and the close-up lands on the arms-wide pose.
