# 008 | Mutiny on the Bounty: Admiralty Sea Chart Copperplate

A voyage stays on a nautical chart, connecting mapped routes and shipboard events within one continuous shot.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Engraved hatching, rhumb lines, a compass rose, soundings, and hand washes establish a chart's reading conventions. Events occur inside a ship illustration while the surrounding map remains visible. The support provides texture and explains routes and spatial relationships.

The camera slides from the whole chart into its illustration; a progressively drawn track extends local action to a geographical scale. The author keeps the sequence on paper throughout. This invites comparison of how diagrams connect to concrete scenes and when a medium's boundary remains visible.

### Original author's design explanation

An 18th-century Admiralty sea chart engraved in copperplate, with rhumb lines, compass roses, soundings and thin hand colour; the Bounty is a ship vignette on the sheet. The film is a voyage, so the whole film stays on the chart.

### Complete original style prompt

A late-18th-century copperplate map, default an Admiralty sea chart (inland: a survey map). Fine burin hatching and cross-hatching (lines ~1/1200 of frame width) give all tone; a minimum line width makes small detail darken, not vanish. Ink #1C1610 on laid paper #EFE3C4, light foxing, folds. Thin hand colour: water #9EBCBC along edges, land #B8C08C; the hero the story centres on (e.g. #D97757) is the one strong colour. Rhumb lines from 32-point roses, a graduated border, hachured hills, cartouche, one ornament (e.g. a sea monster), serif lettering. Put the action in an unboxed engraved vignette fading into the chart, filling 60-75% of frame height at the end of the move. Camera: one 5-6 s glide from the whole sheet into the vignette, chart still around it; a dotted route inks itself in. Avoid: cropping the paper, heavy washes, stiff figures.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. chart | 2.5 seconds

One continuous move: from the whole chart, with the Bounty sailing her dotted track, the camera glides in to the ship vignette, rhumb lines and roses still around it; on deck Clawd swings the cutlass up and points Bligh away as the launch falls astern and its track to Timor inks itself across the sheet.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd is Fletcher Christian in a bicorne worn athwartships, cutlass in hand. Following the v6 note, the film never leaves the chart: one glide, no cuts. The cutlass goes up on beat 4 and levels at the launch a moment later with a glint, while the launch drifts astern through the last beats.
