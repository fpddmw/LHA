# Cyberpunk anime: urban setting and graphic characters

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

A basic proposal combining neon urbanism, technical clothing, and animation, useful for distinguishing world setting from representation.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Strong color separation, clear contours, readable panels, and simplified faces establish graphic expression within a dense technical city. These are separable choices: the setting supplies content, while animation changes how the image is formed.

A new proposal can decide how the graphic treatment organizes the technical setting: strong contours may separate figures from dense panels, while simplified faces and selective color contrasts direct attention. Reading the two components together helps the model develop a particular animated city rather than concatenate cyberpunk and anime labels.

The source has no complete shot list or experiment results; identity preservation is an intended outcome. Compare it with the cyberpunk city card to identify changes introduced by animation, or with other animation cases to explore alternative expressions of a similar subject.
