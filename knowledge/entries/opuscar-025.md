# 025 | The Greatest Show on Earth: Strobridge Circus Lithograph

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md)

A circus poster's radiating perspective and simultaneous actions become a sequence of searching, procession, and a climactic catch.

## Editorial reading and design relationships

Four-color lithography, heavy outlines, local stippling, and exaggerated perspective support a crowded spectacle. Many recognizable figures share the layout, while the principal action becomes a convergence point for perspective and attention.

The camera tilts upward to find a high figure, tracks the procession, and cuts during action to the catch. The poster's decisive composition becomes a timed destination. This is a complete example of turning image composition into a video process.

### Original author's design explanation

A Strobridge circus lithograph: flat chrome yellow, vermilion and cobalt with heavy black outlines, screened and slightly off register on grainy stone. DeMille's big top becomes a three-ring poster, with Clawd as the Great Sebastian making the poster's trapeze catch.

### Complete original style prompt

A circus chromolithograph after the Strobridge Lithographing Co. (c. 1890). Flat ink, heavy black outlines (about 1/300 of frame width), crescent shadows, light from upper left. Four stones: chrome yellow #F4C416, vermilion #D62C24, cobalt #1E4EA0, key black #181210 on warm cream paper #EBD9B4; overprints and tints give only orange, green, brown, tan, pale blue, grey. Solids print flat; screens (dots about 1/250 of frame width) only on gradients (sky, skin, ground), each stone at its own angle, 1-3 px off register, stone grain. The hero the story centres on is solid yellow under solid vermilion, a flat orange (e.g. #D97757). Exaggerated perspective, radial lines converging on the action, several actions at once; any crowd is tiny individual figures, though a lone figure works. Camera: tilt up, lateral dolly, cut on action. Avoid: off-palette colours, dot-only crowds, a screened, pinkish hero.

### All shots in their original order

#### 1. tent | 0.8 seconds

Wide in the big top: three rings, the bowl of spectators, elephants on the track; tilt up the canvas to Clawd hanging from the highest trapeze.

#### 2. elephants | 0.6 seconds

Lateral dolly along the elephant parade, trunk to tail under spangled riders, past the ring curb and the front-row heads.

#### 3. trapeze | 1.0 seconds

Cut on the action to the catch: Clawd, hanging by its knees in a spangled cape, holds the pink flyer in one hand and flings the other arm out to the crowd as the swing carries them right; the camera follows with a slight push and roll.

### Direction and handoff

Clawd plays the Great Sebastian: a sequinned cape with a gold scalloped edge, upside down on the trapeze. The tilt up finds it on the bar, the elephant dolly keeps the parade moving through the middle, and the cut lands on the catch, timed so the swing is on the poster's right-hand side, arm flung out to the crowd.
