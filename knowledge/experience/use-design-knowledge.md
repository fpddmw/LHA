# Use design knowledge where it changes the work

[Experience map](README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md) · [Design application](../guides/design-expression.md)

Version 1. These are common-sense reading and adaptation preferences, informed by the structure of this corpus. No measured retrieval-efficiency improvement is claimed.

## Match reading to what is unresolved

For a clear local request, direct action can be appropriate. For an open style or unfamiliar combination, compare sources until a useful interpretation emerges. A long request may already contain most of its design; a short request may leave it open. Reading depth follows the unresolved question, not the instruction's length.

Begin with a comparative guide when the differences between possible directions matter. Open a complete case when a relevant relationship is already known. Follow source links when a historical claim or a detail affects the decision. Search results identify places to read; snippets do not establish the complete design or experiment.

## Borrow relationships with their dependencies

Use a case to understand how its image forms, how its parts relate and why a choice serves its subject. Adapt those relationships to the current video. Transparent color over visible pencil can inform a bright interior without bringing along the original prairie, sunset palette or journal labels. A technical interior can inform a sports arena without assigning laboratory roles to the players.

When combining directions, look for a relationship that makes them work together. Circuit-like embroidery and a shared material/light system are possible authored choices for a technological fantasy request; no particular mapping is compulsory. The [combination guide](../guides/combination.md) and complete cases provide comparisons while leaving the proposal to the model.

## Reuse understanding and revise it when results matter

Previously read sources and useful notes can remain available across generations. There is no reason to repeat the same reading merely because another video was produced. Revisit a source when a result exposes a consequential uncertainty or suggests a different interpretation.

The corpus's artistic cases explain visual relationships, often from code and three-dimensional rendering. They do not prove that a video model will execute the same design. Consult [model experience](../model-experience/bernini-r.md) for scoped execution observations. A model's failure can change the application strategy without changing what the source author wrote.

Retain a useful proposal even when one realization fails, while remaining willing to simplify or change it when evidence warrants. The cost of extra reading and generation should serve the current task. Record actual use through tool receipts and ordinary notes; no bibliography quota or mandatory adaptation form applies.
