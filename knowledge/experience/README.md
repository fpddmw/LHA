> Historical scope — 2026-11-01: This is the legacy experience catalog entrance. Current creative_loop_v1 tasks resolve experience-map to [the curated entrance](curated/README.md). The four stable topic paths now serve as concise routes to current manuals and guides; old saved snapshots retain their recorded content.

[Knowledge map](../README.md) · [Reading manual](../READING.md) · [Recorded model experience](../model-experience/bernini-r.md)

These readable skill topics help choose a useful next action. Their initial recommendations are **common-sense starting preferences**, available for immediate use. They are not demonstrated route advantages. Individual experimental findings are labeled and linked to their conditions. The Planner decides whether an experience applies to the current task; these pages do not drive a programmatic router.

| Topic | When it can help |
|---|---|
| [Choose an editing input](choose-editing-input.md) | Decide whether to restart from the original, continue a candidate, revisit an earlier version, or sample another result |
| [Express an edit for its actual input](express-edits.md) | Write a complete target or a focused change without assuming the renderer sees the planning conversation |
| [Inspect and compare results](inspect-and-compare.md) | Locate a consequential problem, compare alternatives, and decide whether more evidence is useful |
| [Use design knowledge](use-design-knowledge.md) | Decide what is worth reading and adapt a source relationship to the current scene |

Semantic understanding and design sources remain available through the main map. A topic can lead to either group, to another experience, or to a complete experimental record. Reading has no fixed order or quota. A clear narrow task may need no additional reading.

The initial edition is version 1. Common-sense preferences can be narrowed, strengthened or replaced by subsequent evidence. Run records retain the material actually supplied. Proposed updates stay separate from active material until reviewed. Stable tool instructions and original source accounts have their own maintenance process; a video score does not rewrite them.
