# Renderer profiles: starting preferences and recurring traps

Draft revision 2. Use the profile for the selected renderer to prioritize design and inspection, then follow the relevant experience for a fuller account. These profiles describe recorded setups; adjust their working defaults from the actual candidate.

## Bernini

Recorded setup: Bernini-R 1.3B, 40-step `v2v_apg`, maximum image size 848, empty negative prompt, no prompt enhancer. The seven-task batch used native time axes. Per-output receipts preserve revisions, seeds and timing.

**Starting preference: make the visual construction concrete, allocate changes to the right parts, and use intact candidates to develop compatible additions. Watch activity and identity when a new scene asks the renderer to reinterpret people.**

- **Medium conversion benefits from a chosen visual system.** The storybook probe became clearly illustrative with pencil contours, washes, soft shadows and paper texture; a short etching label already worked. Prefer a compact construction of the intended medium over piling up style adjectives. If the first output delivers the requested interpretation, put attention on the actual remaining requirements rather than automatic elaboration. [Style realization](entries/style-realization.md).
- **Theme objects can leave the old location intact.** Space views and clothing appeared while gym walls and equipment persisted; the metallic train initially remained in an ordinary crossing. Map the location-defining surfaces and sightlines into the new setting. F006/F007 show useful continuation of a pavilion/train foundation; F004's explicit remapping still stalled. Strong F001/F004 v1 environment changes also occurred, so inspect the actual carrier of persistence instead of assuming an inability to replace rooms. [Environment](entries/environment-replacement.md), [input choice](entries/input-conditioning.md).
- **Identity descriptions can reconstruct people.** F002/F003/F005 lost identity despite explicit preservation and detailed features. Keep individual descriptions concise, preserve the intended outfit, reduce unnecessary reconstruction around faces, and favor an input with the needed person when identity is central. F004/F006 demonstrate that useful identity retention is possible amid substantial edits. [Instance identity](entries/instance-identity.md).
- **A plausible genre can reorganize the event.** The offered microphone became self-directed; casino appearance displaced a mahjong layout. Give the genre visual scope around the requested activity and specify its discriminating relations. F005 improved its apparatus through a sharper division of playing system and exterior frame; F003's explicit recipient still failed. When a clear relation remains unrealized, change the competing realization rather than extending the recital. [Activity](entries/activity-relations.md), [realization change](entries/realization-change.md).
- **Emission can bind to a nearby support.** Blue light repeatedly appeared below the table. Prefer describing the intended perimeter in relation to unlit supports; F004 v2 gained the intended edge. If the relation was already explicit, reconsider geometry and conditioning before another stronger adjective. [Part binding](entries/part-binding.md).
- **Motion transitions expose duplication and fading.** Basketball/phone state changes produced extra instances, and clothing attributes disappeared later in clips. Tie the object to its existing interaction and inspect retrieval, release and view changes. Sequence language remains useful when it clarifies the actual action. [Object continuity](entries/object-continuity.md), [time binding](entries/time-binding.md).
- **Surface success can hide a failed operation.** Glitch-colored blocks did not establish displacement of scene-bearing strips. Lower the priority of repeated wording-only retries and choose a genuinely different construction or retain a sufficient treatment, according to the request. [Realization change](entries/realization-change.md).

## Ditto

Recorded setup: Wan2.1-VACE-14B with Ditto LoRA, 50 steps, CFG 5, LoRA alpha 1, tiled bf16, empty negative prompt, no enhancer. Evidence is from the historical 2026-10-30 runs; no new Ditto batch was executed in this maintenance pass.

**Starting preference: use the model's observed strength in broad setting changes, and spend early attention on foreground identity and structural costs. Treat precise candidate shape repair as a risky operation requiring clear instance anchoring and a useful fallback input.**

- **Strong environment replacement can accompany foreground reconstruction.** On the shared table-tennis source, space-station walls and Earth views arrived early while faces and eyewear changed. Focus the proposal on compatible foreground treatment instead of escalating background instructions after the setting is already convincing. Compare the actual subject state before continuation. [Environment](entries/environment-replacement.md), [identity](entries/instance-identity.md).
- **Age and accessory descriptions can backfire.** Historical descriptions of age, hair and glasses produced changed age or eyewear; one result recovered glasses without the person. Prefer preserving the individual through input and edit scope over expanding a facial inventory. Treat a required headwear change as a design problem to reconcile with identity. [Instance identity](entries/instance-identity.md).
- **Candidate shape correction can add an object.** The compact-dome request produced a second dome. Prefer an earlier intact input for an important instance repair, and anchor the existing landmark to its neighbors and footprint. When a later pass introduces overlays, an earlier styled candidate can retain useful appearance without that artifact, as in the glitch branch from v1. [Part binding](entries/part-binding.md), [input choice](entries/input-conditioning.md).
- **Ornament can succeed while silhouette fails.** Silk, palette and embroidery appeared across the costume attempts while standing collars and broad horizontal headwear stayed absent. Inspect the requested construction separately. Favor a different structural realization over a repeated wording-only attempt when the shape remains missing. [Style realization](entries/style-realization.md).
- **Global warmth can dominate faces.** A candidate correction for yellow-orange skin intensified the cast. Prefer establishing distinct facial fill and background practicals in the initial lighting design. If the cast is already severe, compare a return to usable skin evidence with continued correction rather than merely demanding natural skin under the same global gold treatment. [Lighting](entries/lighting-integration.md), [wanted state](entries/wanted-state.md).
- **Embellishment and glitch cues can exceed or miss the task.** Ornate architectural additions may suit a broad etching request but damage an exact landmark task. Glitch streaks and overlays leave scene displacement unconfirmed. Choose delivery by the user's actual objective and retained alternatives rather than treating every extra ornament as a defect or every genre cue as full compliance. [Continuation value](entries/continuation-value.md).
