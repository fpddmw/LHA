# Renderer entrances

Current recommendations below come from the same entry summaries as the question map. Read the complete account for renderer-specific evidence and counterexamples. [Historical renderer profiles](evidence/renderer-profile-20261102.md) retain the recorded setups and earlier interpretations; current entry revisions own current advice.

## Bernini

- [EX-semantic-reconstruction](entries/semantic-reconstruction.md): When the target world changes the event, rebuild its purpose and causal relationships. Use useful source performance material and remove preservation demands that keep the old activity in place.
- [EX-design-conflict](entries/design-conflict.md): Remove an unnecessary preservation demand before sacrificing a requested design feature. When appearance and action are both central, stage them together so the target interaction remains readable.
- [EX-activity-relations](entries/activity-relations.md): Organize participants and object functions around the target event. Preserve a required interaction; reconstruct an obsolete purpose with a readable new exchange rather than merely retaining the same nouns.
- [EX-instance-identity](entries/instance-identity.md): For identity-sensitive edits, preserve individual correspondence through useful input evidence and scoped design. Allow the role and behavior to change when requested; concise identity wording avoids constructing a substitute face.
- [EX-object-continuity](entries/object-continuity.md): Bind continuity to the target participant and event. Use source-linked roles for retained action; establish new contacts and progression for adapted action, and inspect occlusion and release where they matter.
- [EX-connected-scope](entries/connected-scope.md): Group changes that share a structural, optical or semantic dependency. An adapted event can connect people, prop functions and environment; unrelated repairs may need a different scope or input.
- [EX-input-conditioning](entries/input-conditioning.md): Continue a candidate with useful target achievements. Recover required lost information from an earlier input; an authorized change of source activity can be progress rather than damage.
- [EX-realization-change](entries/realization-change.md): Reconsider an unnecessary commitment before retrying its execution. For a required operation, prefer a changed construction or input after explicit failure; a new material can still preserve the old function.
- [EX-part-binding](entries/part-binding.md): Clarify the intended carrier relative to its neighbors. Anchor an existing instance for a required repair; describe the new construction when the task replaces its form or function.
- [EX-wanted-state](entries/wanted-state.md): Specify the positive visual relationship and, for event reconstruction, the purpose that should replace the old one. Resolve the surrounding conflict instead of repeatedly suppressing its symptom.
- [EX-style-realization](entries/style-realization.md): Choose a coherent visible system and the event logic required by the request. World reconstruction can need new behavior; atmosphere and ornament do not substitute for required structures or relationships.
- [EX-environment-replacement](entries/environment-replacement.md): Transform the location-defining planes, sightlines and functions together. Replace an obsolete use when the target needs it; additional theme objects or new materials can leave the old scene intact.
- [EX-lighting-integration](entries/lighting-integration.md): Design receiving surfaces, fill and reflections that produce the desired atmosphere. Use light to make the target event readable; lamps alone can leave old illumination or obsolete action emphasis intact.
- [EX-time-binding](entries/time-binding.md): Inspect the target event and appearance through turns, contacts and responses. Preserve original timing where required; evaluate adapted behavior on its own progression rather than its resemblance to the old action.
- [EX-continuation-value](entries/continuation-value.md): Favor delivery when the target is adequately realized. Reassess inherited preservation complaints; spend another attempt on an important target gap and compare against retained alternatives.
