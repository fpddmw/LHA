# EX-C-craft: Craft and material worlds need a construction clause for every object

**In a craft world the medium is made of pieces: studs and baseplates, folded cardstock, stitched felt, cut-paper layers. Each plane needs a clause describing how it is assembled from that material. A figure in the material inside an untouched photographic yard reads as a collage.**

## What was observed

Only one craft task is in the train evidence: the brick world (026). Direct, the PE loop and the Planner's first prompts converted the figure and the beam and left the truck, hedge, trees, barrier and sky photographic, and the judge's low coherence reasons name that mixture. A Planner prompt that gave each plane its own brick construction converted the whole scene; its remaining defects were a photographic panel in the first 0.6 s and, later, a cluttered corner ([plane record](../evidence/v5-lego-planes.md)). See [EX-P-every-plane](P-every-plane.md) and [EX-P-medium-logic](P-medium-logic.md).

## Not yet tested (hypotheses)

The other craft tasks in the train split (016 paper pop-up, 019 shadow-play cut paper, 031 felt miniature, 045 paper cut-out) have no scores in this evidence. The same approach is expected to apply: name joints, seams, layers and how light reads on the material. Treat these as suggestions to test.

**Evidence status.** Strong working pattern for one task; hypothesis for the family.
