# EX-design-conflict: Resolve conflicts between the target design and necessary action

Revision 5. Approved for use; goal-directed semantic revision authorized on 2026-11-02.

**When appearance and action compete, identify which commitments belong to the requested result. Remove an unnecessary preservation demand before sacrificing a requested design feature. When both are central, reshape their staging so the target interaction remains readable.**

## Locate the authored conflict

A long robe can express a magical character while covering the chair that makes a seated reading posture clear. In the study trace, repeating the chair description left the conflict; a later fitted jacket made seating more readable. This supplies a useful design distinction: a silhouette occupies space that may carry an important relation. Apply that distinction to the target event, whose contacts and supports may differ from the source.

In F001, flowing wide sleeves came from the user, while precise basketball choreography came from Planner. The later proposal exposed the hands through open cuffs to protect dribbling and shooting. For the clarified world reconstruction, prioritizing that invented preservation requirement kept the wrong activity and added difficult hand/ball dependencies. Reconsider the activity before redesigning the sleeves. The [case interpretation](../evidence/semantic-goal-review-20261102.md) separates this goal error from the recorded rendering defects.

## Give necessary relations a workable visual form

When the requested scene still depends on reading, playing an instrument or offering an object, preserve the support, contact or response that makes it intelligible. Adjust drape, pose, placement, light or viewing emphasis to express both the appearance and the activity. A requested garment deserves a coherent realization, not automatic replacement by a simpler cut.

For a reconstructed event, use the gesture, cloth movement, orientation or counterpart response that carries its new meaning. A hand can be naturally concealed when visible progression still establishes the action. Treat hidden required detail as unknown rather than as a successful repair. Choose simplicity in dependencies that serve no purpose, while retaining a substantial readable event.

## Inspect the relationship after the appearance changes

F003's narrower-sleeve proposal accompanied a more recognizable guitar, while microphone roles and identities remained wrong. Recovering a prop's outline did not restore the whole performance. Check the target event itself after resolving an obstruction; a cleaner image can still express the wrong action. [Connected scope](connected-scope.md) helps group changes that share the same cause.
