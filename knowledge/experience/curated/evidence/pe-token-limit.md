# PE prompts refused for length (preview batch, train samples)

Source: the saved PE token-limit evidence from the four-sample preview, restricted to train samples. Two first-round PE prompts were refused before rendering: 340 words (brick-world long) and 351 words (brick-world short). Each error read "Prompt exceeds tokenizer limit; automatic truncation is disabled" and recorded that no render started. A Planner prompt of 313 words in the brick-world long task passed. Only the fact of the limit and its word-count scale is used by the entries.
