# Express an edit for its actual input

[Experience entrance](curated/README.md) · [Understand and design](../creative-loop/UNDERSTAND_AND_DESIGN.md) · [Design expression](../guides/design-expression.md)

Write the visible arrangement the selected input should become. The renderer receives its supplied input and prompt; references to earlier notes or comparisons need to be expressed as visible content when that information is absent from the input.

Use the [design-expression guide](../guides/design-expression.md) to turn a style into connected visual choices. Pencil visible through transparent wash specifies how two materials share a figure. A luminous strip on the upper tabletop perimeter identifies a different target from illuminated supports. Specificity is useful when it resolves such a choice.

Use [Understand and design](../creative-loop/UNDERSTAND_AND_DESIGN.md) to determine the room for invention in the request. Develop open style choices; carry a detailed supplied design through to its intended subjects and parts. Use [Understand and revise](../creative-loop/UNDERSTAND_AND_REVISE.md) when the relation was already explicit but the result missed it: the earlier prompt helps distinguish a new realization from a stronger restatement.

The [Bernini probe](../model-experience/bernini-r.md) provides the historical basis for concrete style elaboration. The storybook case gained a clear illustration treatment with pencil-and-watercolor detail; the short etching label already worked. These are task-specific observations with length and information content changing together. Approved renderer and expression experience belongs in the [experience entrance](curated/README.md).
