Reimagine the setting as a cloud-wreathed immortal realm in the style of Zu Warriors (2001). The two people become cultivators dueling among floating stone platforms and distant celestial palaces. The original offensive and defensive interaction becomes a contest of spells or magical artifacts; clothing, props, and the purpose of the space all belong to the xianxia world. Flowing mist, interwoven warm and cool magical light, and fluttering robes create fantastical depth, with specific moves developing naturally from the original bodily actions.
