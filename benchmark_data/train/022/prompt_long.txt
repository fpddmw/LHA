Render the person and sunflower garden as a Monet-style Impressionist oil painting. Loose short strokes and warm and cool dabs weave together flowers, foliage, and paths. The person carries the container among the flowers, with their outline merging into luminous air. Soft yellow-green, pale violet, and blue shadows suggest flickering sunlight. Brushwork follows walking movements and plant forms, creating a light, lively summer garden.
