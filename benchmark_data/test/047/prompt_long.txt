Transform the restaurant into a Nautilus dining room in the style of Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Seas. Riveted copper walls, curved ribs, and thick-glass portholes enclose the dining space. Diners and staff continue their activities, with tableware and lamps integrated into a Victorian steam-mechanical aesthetic. Deep blue water and fish silhouettes outside the portholes cast light into the room, mingling with warm golden lamps to evoke a secluded, lavish underwater voyage.
