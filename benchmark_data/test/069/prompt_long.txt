Transform the hall into a Devdas-style palace celebration. Carved columns, marigold garlands, and rows of oil lamps surround the dance floor. Dancers wear jewel-colored embroidered skirts; hems, gold jewelry, and veils flow with the original circle-dance rhythm, while spectators and companions join the festive setting. Rich red and gold, layered candlelight, and lustrous fabrics create an exuberant musical-film image.
