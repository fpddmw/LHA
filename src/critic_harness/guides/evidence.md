# Choosing evidence

Status: initial observation guidance. Revise from reviewed calibration cases, keeping operating contracts and formal scoring standards versioned separately.

Overview frames answer broad style, layout and entity questions. A crop answers a localized question and should retain enough surroundings to establish which part is being inspected. Native timestamps and conditioning correspondence describe sampling, not proof that generated motion matches the original.

For motion, use continuous native frames over a relevant interval. Short windows keep small objects and transitions inspectable. Inspect the source too when evaluating retained motion. Distinguish event identity, action ordering, contact relationships and exact trajectories; exact trajectory claims require sufficient spatial and temporal evidence. A naturally quiet source should remain quiet without a generic dynamic-expression penalty.

To assess full-clip temporal stability, cover the full interval. Local hand crops can establish a local contact problem but leave background flicker unresolved. Dense-frame availability permits inspection; it does not guarantee that the model resolved a tiny ball or a brief occlusion. Report unknown when visibility remains insufficient. Do not infer missing frames or silently repeat the final frame.

Look for appearance flicker, structural jumps, unsupported object births/disappearances and motion freezes separately. Intentional glitch and animated texture can be stylistic; judge their effects against the requested treatment and activity. A high adjacent-frame feature similarity can also occur in frozen footage.

Compare alternatives over the same question and corresponding time range. Unequal native rates need not imply an action error. A scene can have improved table-edge placement while identity remains damaged; preserve both observations. A/B order disagreements warrant review rather than a confident ranking.

Calibration should check missed defects, false alarms on valid stylization, consistent partial labels, accurate locations and appropriate unknowns. Human/independent review anchors the check. Self-reported confidence alone is not measured reliability. Small diagnostic fixtures are labeled separately from actual generated videos.
